#!/usr/bin/env python3
"""cvt9 REGISTRATION-TIME DERIVATIONS (CORRECTIONS 249).  Reads ONLY landed records (probe.jsonl and the raw .out TEST
lines of cvt1-cvt6 and cpl2, all LANDED) and the corpus through analysis/corpus_exclusions.filter_rows.  Nothing here
reads a cvt9 run.  What it prints is copied into analysis/cVT9_dosewindow_score.py as LITERALS; that scorer's selftest
re-runs the pure rules (RULE P via cvt4's module, RULE M, RULE E) on the host's records and requires equality.

THE QUESTION (247.13 R2; 240.4(2), 246.4(4)).  On PlainNet18_c100 at cvt1's cell, idx 50 (layer4.1.bn2.weight) held on
MUTE's replayed large trajectory (tri:9428) from init to the end puts the arm at the k01 location, with the complement
free (cvt4 / cvt6 HOLDHIGH) and with the complement forced open-loop onto HEAD's measured path (cvt6 HIGHHEADPATH).
WHEN does that large step size do the damage, and how does the damage scale with its DOSE?  cvt9 keeps the complement
forced onto HEADPATH in EVERY held arm (cvt6: LOWHEADPATH 65.02 AT-HEAD, so the forced HEADPATH costs nothing at LOW),
so every held arm differs from every other ONLY in 50's schedule.

THE THREE RULES (written here, before any cvt9 run)
  RULE P   (237.3, cvt4_registration_derivations.py, imported UNEDITED): P = round((median peak - b0) / ms).
           P_HIGH 9428 (MUTE / MUTE50 shared beta, 6 runs), P_HEAD 5041 (HEAD's isolated 50, 9 runs).  RESDOSE replays
           cvt7's P_HIGH 8609 (RULE P on ResNet18_c100 k01's shared beta at ciso1's cell, 243.3(2)) EXACTLY, so its
           dose reading lines up with cvt7's HOLDHIGH and cvt8's rungs; the ResNet records (ciso2) are not all on every
           host, so 8609 is read from the registered cVT7 scorer and re-derived by cvt7's own derivation where they are.
  RULE M   (MIDDOSE).  P_MID = (P_HEAD + P_HIGH + 1) // 2 = 7235: the midpoint, in log step size, of the two registered
           PlainNet 50-replays whose outcome is landed -- tri:5041 (HOLDHEAD, cvt4: AT-HEAD, 64.67) and tri:9428
           (HOLDHIGH / HIGHHEADPATH, cvt4 / cvt6: AT-K01) -- rounded half UP (7234.5 -> 7235).  It is RULE P's formula
           applied to that midpoint peak: (peak_mid - b0) / ms = 7234.5.  Peak -6.5805, r at the peak ~4,540 = 9x HOLDHEAD's
           and 1/9 of HOLDHIGH's: a bisection rung.  RESDOSE (8609) then sits between MID and HIGH.
  RULE E   (the window cut).  n_E = P_HIGH + 1 = 9429, the update after which tri:9428 reaches its peak (epoch 18.858).
           EARLY = WINDOW_HOLD 0:9429 (50 on tri:9428 for updates n < n_E, then the floor); LATE = WINDOW_HOLD 9429:end
           (50 at the floor for n < n_E, then on the REST of tri:9428: the peak and the fall).  Pointwise EARLY + LATE
           cover tri:9428 exactly once.  WHY THE PEAK: the triangle is symmetric in log step size about n_E, so the two
           halves carry the SAME multiset of step sizes (the rise b0 -> peak vs the fall peak -> b0; LATE additionally
           has the peak update itself and the fall below b0 to the clamp, together < 0.2 % of the integrated step size,
           S3) and the same 100x-floor sub-window length (~12.0 epochs each) -- EARLY vs LATE is a TIMING contrast at
           matched dose.  The landed TEST splits (the line from which the large-50 arm stays > 2 pp under its rescued
           twin: cvt4 HOLDHIGH vs HEAD, cvt6 HIGHHEADPATH vs LOWHEADPATH, cvt6 HOLDHIGH vs HEAD) end at or before update
           9000 < n_E for all three arm means and 8 of 9 seeds; the one exception, cvt6 HOLDHIGH s97 (free complement),
           splits at line 18 (updates 9001-9500), the epoch that contains n_E (S3).  For the forced-complement pair cvt9
           replicates (HIGHHEADPATH vs LOWHEADPATH) every split -- mean and each seed -- ends inside EARLY.  A cut AT the split
           (update ~8500) was considered: EARLY would get ~20 % of the integrated step size and LATE the peak, so a LATE
           stall would not say 'late' -- it would say 'more dose'.

THE REPLAY FILE (HEADPATH) -- reused, not re-derived.  Every held cvt9 arm forces the complement onto
patches/cvt6_headpath.json (sha 74be71fa..., CORRECTIONS 242.3(2)) BYTE FOR BYTE, so LOWHEADPATH / HIGHHEADPATH are
exact replicates of cvt6's arms.  ELIGIBILITY RULE for a HEADPATH source (stated here, 246.9's lesson): every landed
100-epoch HEAD run (`sets:1-49,51-53/layer4.1.bn2.weight`, VOTE_W / BETA_HOLD / COMP_HOLD off) of PlainNet18_c100 at
cvt1's cell with 500 probe records.  Today that is 18 runs: cpl2 s75-77, cvt1 s78-80, cvt2 s87-89, cvt3 s84-86, cvt4
s90-92, cvt6 s96-98 (cvt5's HEAD runs are 300-epoch runs: out of rule).  The registered file is the median of 12 of them
(246.9).  S4 prints the 18-run median against the file; the file is NOT regenerated (a different path would break the
replication and every between-batch comparison with cvt6).

Sections:
  S1  RULE P re-run on this host; P_RES read from the registered cVT7 scorer
  S2  RULE M and the four dose rungs (5041 landed, 7235, 8609, 9428): peak, peak / clamp epoch, 100x-floor window,
      integrated step size, r(15-30)
  S3  RULE E: the two halves' integrated step size, peak, 100x-floor sub-windows; the landed TEST splits, their updates;
      the rescued twin's TEST at Epoch line 17 (LATE's onset level, a registered bar term's prior)
  S4  HEADPATH: the eligibility rule, the 18 eligible runs, the 18-run median vs the registered file
  S5  G-BITE non-vacuity on landed cvt6 records: HIGHHEADPATH's 50 read as MIDDOSE / RESDOSE / EARLY / LATE and
      LOWHEADPATH's 50 read as EARLY / LATE -- each misses by >> BH_TOL
  S6  the meta-optimiser's state under a window (dead state), stated
  S7  the noise floor on the current corpus, 227.6's definitions, through corpus_exclusions.filter_rows

  python3 analysis/cvt9_registration_derivations.py <runs dir holding cvt1/ .. cvt6/, cpl2/ and the .out files> [<all_runs.csv>]
"""
import csv
import glob
import hashlib
import json
import math
import os
import re
import sys

HERE = os.path.dirname(os.path.abspath(__file__))
REPO = os.path.dirname(HERE)
sys.path.insert(0, HERE)
import cvt4_registration_derivations as DV4      # registered at 237, imported UNEDITED (b0, ms, v_of, RULE P, Lion)
sys.path.pop(0)

B0, MS, LO, HI, BH_TOL, f32 = DV4.B0, DV4.MS, DV4.LO, DV4.HI, DV4.BH_TOL, DV4.f32
N_RECORDS = 500
UPE = 500                                          # meta updates per epoch (50,000 / batch 100)
TOTAL_UPDATES = 50000
REPLAY_ID = "cvt6_headpath"
REPLAY_SHA = "74be71fa524ad0122d1408e01dd2b633b004b2e27228393fe6e593f494358a5d"
ELIGIBLE_HEAD = (("cpl2", (75, 76, 77)), ("cvt1", (78, 79, 80)), ("cvt2", (87, 88, 89)), ("cvt3", (84, 85, 86)),
                 ("cvt4", (90, 91, 92)), ("cvt6", (96, 97, 98)))
EPTR_RE = re.compile(r"Epoch\s+(\d+),\s*Train Accuracy:\s*([0-9.]+|nan)\s*%,\s*Test Accuracy:\s*([0-9.]+|nan)")


def rule_m(p_head, p_high):
    """RULE M: the log-step-size midpoint of the two landed replays, rounded half up."""
    return (p_head + p_high + 1) // 2


def rule_e(p_high):
    """RULE E: the update after which tri:P_HIGH peaks.  EARLY = [0, n_E), LATE = [n_E, end)."""
    return p_high + 1


def p_res_registered():
    txt = open(os.path.join(HERE, "cVT7_grouphold_score.py")).read()
    m = re.search(r"^P_HIGH = (\d+)\s", txt, re.M)
    return int(m.group(1)) if m else None


def win_value(n, P, n0, n1):
    """the windowed schedule as float32: tri_P(n) inside [n0, n1) (n1 None = no end), the floor outside."""
    if n >= n0 and (n1 is None or n < n1):
        return DV4.v_of(n, P)
    return f32(LO)


def sched_stats(fn, n_max=TOTAL_UPDATES + 1):
    vals = [fn(n) for n in range(0, n_max)]
    r = [math.exp(v + 15.0) for v in vals]
    pk = max(range(len(vals)), key=lambda i: vals[i])
    over = [n for n in range(len(vals)) if r[n] > 100.0]
    clamp = next((n for n in range(pk, len(vals)) if all(v == f32(LO) for v in vals[n:n + 1]) and vals[n] == f32(LO)), None)
    win1530 = [r[n] for n in range(15 * UPE, 30 * UPE)]
    return {"peak": vals[pk], "peak_n": pk, "clamp_n": clamp, "int_r": sum(r), "over100": (over[0], over[-1]) if over else None,
            "r1530": sum(win1530) / len(win1530), "vals": vals}


def out_files(runs, batch, arm, seed):
    return sorted(glob.glob(os.path.join(runs, "%s-%s-s%d-*.out" % (batch, arm, seed))))


def test_curve(path):
    eps = {}
    for a, b, c in EPTR_RE.findall(open(path, errors="replace").read()):
        eps[int(a)] = (float(b), float(c))
    return eps


def arm_curve(runs, batch, arm, seeds):
    per = []
    for s in seeds:
        fs = out_files(runs, batch, arm, s)
        if len(fs) != 1:
            raise SystemExit("!!! %s-%s-s%d: %d .out files" % (batch, arm, s, len(fs)))
        per.append(test_curve(fs[0]))
    mean = dict((e, sum(p[e][1] for p in per) / len(per)) for e in range(100) if all(e in p for p in per))
    return mean, per


def split_line(x, y):
    """first Epoch line from which x - y stays > 2 pp for good (x, y: line -> TEST)."""
    for e in range(100):
        if all(x[f] - y[f] > 2.0 for f in range(e, 100) if f in x and f in y):
            return e
    return None


def load(runs, batch, arm, seed):
    return DV4.load(runs, batch, arm, seed)


def main():
    runs = sys.argv[1] if len(sys.argv) > 1 else os.path.join(os.path.dirname(REPO), "runs_cluster_b")
    csvpath = sys.argv[2] if len(sys.argv) > 2 else os.path.join(REPO, "results", "all_runs.csv")
    print("cvt9 registration derivations (CORRECTIONS 249)")
    print("  runs %s" % runs)
    print("  b0 = float32(log 1e-6) = %r ; ms = %r ; clamp [%r, %r] ; BH_TOL %r" % (B0, MS, LO, HI, BH_TOL))

    print("\nS1  RULE P (237.3) re-run on this host; RESDOSE's P read from the registered cVT7 scorer (243.3(2))")
    P = DV4.derive_P(runs)
    P_RES = p_res_registered()
    print("  RULE P on this host: P_HIGH %d, P_HEAD %d, P_K01 %d" % (P["HIGH"], P["HEAD"], P["K01"]))
    print("  P_RES (cVT7_grouphold_score.py P_HIGH, RULE P on ResNet18_c100 k01 at ciso1's cell) %s" % P_RES)

    print("\nS2  RULE M and the dose ladder (every rung a max-rate triangle from b0)")
    P_MID = rule_m(P["HEAD"], P["HIGH"])
    mid_exact = ((B0 + MS * P["HEAD"]) + (B0 + MS * P["HIGH"])) / 2.0
    print("  RULE M: P_MID = (P_HEAD + P_HIGH + 1) // 2 = (%d + %d + 1) // 2 = %d   [(peak_mid - b0)/ms = %.4f; peak_mid %.6f]"
          % (P["HEAD"], P["HIGH"], P_MID, (mid_exact - B0) / MS, mid_exact))
    rungs = [("HOLDHEAD (cvt4, landed AT-HEAD)", P["HEAD"]), ("MIDDOSE", P_MID), ("RESDOSE", P_RES), ("HIGH (landed AT-K01)", P["HIGH"]),
             ("k01's own PlainNet trajectory (reference only)", P["K01"])]
    st = {}
    for lab, p in rungs:
        s = sched_stats(lambda n, p=p: DV4.v_of(n, p))
        st[p] = s
        print("  tri:%-5d %-46s peak %.6f after update %d (epoch %6.3f); clamp from update %s (epoch %s); r>100 from epoch %.2f to %.2f;"
              " integrated r %.4g; r(15-30) %.1f; r at peak %.0f"
              % (p, lab, s["peak"], s["peak_n"], s["peak_n"] / float(UPE), s["clamp_n"],
                 "%.2f" % (s["clamp_n"] / float(UPE)) if s["clamp_n"] else "never", s["over100"][0] / float(UPE),
                 s["over100"][1] / float(UPE), s["int_r"], s["r1530"], math.exp(s["peak"] + 15)))
    print("  peak r ratios: MID/HEAD %.2f ; HIGH/MID %.2f ; RES/MID %.2f ; HIGH/RES %.2f ; RES vs PlainNet's own k01 trajectory: +%.3f log units"
          % (math.exp(st[P_MID]["peak"] - st[P["HEAD"]]["peak"]), math.exp(st[P["HIGH"]]["peak"] - st[P_MID]["peak"]),
             math.exp(st[P_RES]["peak"] - st[P_MID]["peak"]), math.exp(st[P["HIGH"]]["peak"] - st[P_RES]["peak"]),
             st[P_RES]["peak"] - st[P["K01"]]["peak"]))
    print("  DISCLOSED: the triangle family moves peak, onset of the fall and duration together (a smaller P peaks earlier and"
          " clamps earlier) -- 'dose' here is this family, the shape every landed trajectory has (237.3(1)).")

    print("\nS3  RULE E: the window cut, from the tri:%d schedule and the landed TEST splits" % P["HIGH"])
    nE = rule_e(P["HIGH"])
    ear = sched_stats(lambda n: win_value(n, P["HIGH"], 0, nE))
    lat = sched_stats(lambda n: win_value(n, P["HIGH"], nE, None))
    full = st[P["HIGH"]]
    print("  n_E = P_HIGH + 1 = %d (epoch %.3f)" % (nE, nE / float(UPE)))
    for lab, s, n0, n1 in (("EARLY  0:%d" % nE, ear, 0, nE), ("LATE   %d:end" % nE, lat, nE, None)):
        excess = sum(math.exp(v + 15.0) - 1.0 for v in s["vals"])
        print("  %-14s peak %.6f at update %d; r>100 from epoch %.2f to %.2f (%.2f epochs); integrated (r - 1) %.6g"
              % (lab, s["peak"], s["peak_n"], s["over100"][0] / float(UPE), s["over100"][1] / float(UPE),
                 (s["over100"][1] - s["over100"][0]) / float(UPE), excess))
    ex_e = sum(math.exp(v + 15.0) - 1.0 for v in ear["vals"])
    ex_l = sum(math.exp(v + 15.0) - 1.0 for v in lat["vals"])
    ex_f = sum(math.exp(v + 15.0) - 1.0 for v in full["vals"])
    print("  integrated (r - 1): EARLY %.6g + LATE %.6g = %.6g vs the whole tri:%d %.6g (difference %.3g); LATE / EARLY = %.5f"
          " (LATE carries the peak update and the fall below b0 to the clamp)"
          % (ex_e, ex_l, ex_e + ex_l, P["HIGH"], ex_f, ex_e + ex_l - ex_f, ex_l / ex_e))
    pw = all(((win_value(n, P["HIGH"], 0, nE) == DV4.v_of(n, P["HIGH"])) != (win_value(n, P["HIGH"], nE, None) == DV4.v_of(n, P["HIGH"])))
             or DV4.v_of(n, P["HIGH"]) == f32(LO) for n in range(0, TOTAL_UPDATES + 2))
    print("  POINTWISE PARTITION: at every update exactly one of EARLY / LATE carries tri:%d's value (or both sit at the floor"
          " where tri itself is at the floor): %s" % (P["HIGH"], pw))
    print("  LANDED TEST SPLITS (Epoch line L = after L+1 epochs, ends at update 500(L+1)); first line from which the rescued"
          " twin stays > 2 pp above the large-50 arm")
    worst_update = 0
    n_in, n_all = 0, 0
    for batch, x, y, seeds in (("cvt4", "HEAD", "HOLDHIGH", (90, 91, 92)), ("cvt6", "LOWHEADPATH", "HIGHHEADPATH", (96, 97, 98)),
                               ("cvt6", "HEAD", "HOLDHIGH", (96, 97, 98))):
        mx, px = arm_curve(runs, batch, x, seeds)
        my, py = arm_curve(runs, batch, y, seeds)
        L = split_line(mx, my)
        per = [split_line(dict((e, a[e][1]) for e in a), dict((e, b[e][1]) for e in b)) for a, b in zip(px, py)]
        ups = [500 * (l + 1) for l in [L] + per if l is not None]
        worst_update = max([worst_update] + ups)
        n_all += len(ups)
        n_in += sum(u < nE for u in ups)
        print("    %s %-11s - %-12s arm mean line %s (ends at update %s); per seed %s; gap at lines 14/15/16/17/18: %s"
              % (batch, x, y, L, 500 * (L + 1) if L is not None else None, per,
                 " / ".join("%+.2f" % (mx[e] - my[e]) for e in (14, 15, 16, 17, 18))))
    print("  splits ending before n_E = %d (inside EARLY): %d of %d (arm means and seeds); the latest ends at update %d"
          % (nE, n_in, n_all, worst_update))
    m6, p6 = arm_curve(runs, "cvt6", "LOWHEADPATH", (96, 97, 98))
    h6, _ = arm_curve(runs, "cvt6", "HIGHHEADPATH", (96, 97, 98))
    k6, _ = arm_curve(runs, "cvt6", "k01", (96, 97, 98))
    print("  LATE's ONSET LEVEL (a registered bar term, read in batch from LATE's own .out): LATE is bitwise LOWHEADPATH's"
          " schedule until update %d, whose last complete Epoch line is 17 (update 9000)." % nE)
    print("    cvt6 prior: LOWHEADPATH arm-mean TEST at line 17 = %.4f (seeds %s); HIGHHEADPATH %.4f; k01 %.4f; line 18: LOWHEADPATH %.4f"
          % (m6[17], " / ".join("%.3f" % p[17][1] for p in p6), h6[17], k6[17], m6[18]))
    print("    so the LATE stall bar max(k01 + 2, onset + 5) would have been max(%.2f, %.2f) = %.2f in cvt6's numbers; AT-CTL bar %.2f"
          % (k6[99] + 2.0, m6[17] + 5.0, max(k6[99] + 2.0, m6[17] + 5.0), m6[99] - 5.0))

    print("\nS4  HEADPATH: the eligibility rule and every eligible landed run")
    body = open(os.path.join(REPO, "patches", REPLAY_ID + ".json"), "rb").read()
    K = json.loads(body.decode())
    print("  registered file patches/%s.json sha %s (== registered: %s); %d knots; source %d runs: %s"
          % (REPLAY_ID, hashlib.sha256(body).hexdigest(), hashlib.sha256(body).hexdigest() == REPLAY_SHA, len(K["n"]),
             len(K["source"]), " ".join(K["source"])))
    trs = []
    for batch, seeds in ELIGIBLE_HEAD:
        for s in seeds:
            recs = load(runs, batch, "HEAD", s)
            steps = [r["step"] for r in recs]
            ok = steps == [100 * k for k in range(N_RECORDS)]
            print("    eligible %s-HEAD-s%d: %d records, steps 0..49900 every 100: %s; in the registered median: %s"
                  % (batch, s, len(recs), ok, "%s-HEAD-s%d" % (batch, s) in K["source"]))
            if ok:
                trs.append([float(r["beta"][0]) for r in recs])
    med = [DV4.median([t[k] for t in trs]) for k in range(N_RECORDS)]
    d = [abs(med[k] - K["v"][k]) for k in range(N_RECORDS)]
    kx = max(range(N_RECORDS), key=lambda k: d[k])
    print("  the %d-run median vs the registered 12-run file: max |diff| %.4f at record %d (epoch %.1f), mean %.4f"
          % (len(trs), d[kx], kx, kx / 5.0, sum(d) / len(d)))
    print("  REUSED BYTE FOR BYTE (not regenerated): LOWHEADPATH and HIGHHEADPATH replicate cvt6's arms exactly, and every"
          " held cvt9 arm shares the one complement schedule, so no contrast depends on which median was replayed.")

    print("\nS5  G-BITE NON-VACUITY on landed cvt6 records: 50's beta read against the cvt9 schedules it must NOT pass")
    worst = {}
    for src_arm, rd in (("HIGHHEADPATH", (("MIDDOSE", P_MID, 0, None), ("RESDOSE", P_RES, 0, None), ("EARLY", P["HIGH"], 0, nE),
                                          ("LATE", P["HIGH"], nE, None))),
                        ("LOWHEADPATH", (("EARLY", P["HIGH"], 0, nE), ("LATE", P["HIGH"], nE, None)))):
        for s in (96, 97, 98):
            recs = load(runs, "cvt6", src_arm, s)
            for lab, p, n0, n1 in rd:
                db = [abs(float(r["beta"][1]) - win_value(r["step"] + 2, p, n0, n1)) for r in recs]
                dp = [abs(float(r["beta_pre"][0][1]) - win_value(r["step"] + 1, p, n0, n1)) for r in recs]
                nb = sum(x > BH_TOL for x in db)
                key = "%s as %s" % (src_arm, lab)
                worst[key] = min(worst.get(key, 1e9), max(db))
                print("    cvt6 %-12s s%d read as %-8s beta misses %3d/500 (max %.4f); beta_pre misses %3d/500"
                      % (src_arm, s, lab, nb, max(db), sum(x > BH_TOL for x in dp)))
            ok_own = all(abs(float(r["beta"][1]) - (DV4.v_of(r["step"] + 2, P["HIGH"]) if src_arm == "HIGHHEADPATH" else f32(LO))) <= BH_TOL
                         for r in recs)
            print("    cvt6 %-12s s%d passes its OWN 50 schedule on all 500 records: %s" % (src_arm, s, ok_own))
    print("  NON-VACUITY minimum over each null of max|dev|: %s (every one >= 100 x BH_TOL: %s)"
          % ("; ".join("%s %.4f" % kv for kv in sorted(worst.items())), all(v >= 100 * BH_TOL for v in worst.values())))

    print("\nS6  UNDER A WINDOW: the meta-optimiser's state, stated")
    for ln in (
        "  Lion_meta_update runs UNCHANGED on the 2-vector; PATCH_CLIP clamps; PATCH_BETAHOLD writes tri_P(n) on group 1;",
        "  PATCH_COMPHOLD writes HEADPATH on group 0; PATCH_WINDOWHOLD then writes the floor on group 1 OUTSIDE [n0, n1).",
        "  Both momentum entries keep the unpatched recursion on their own raw z and are DEAD STATE (their only reader is",
        "  their own Lion step, overwritten on the same update, from init to the last update).  The windowed arms are open",
        "  loop exactly as cvt6's forced arms: the applied beta vector is a function of the update count only",
        "  (tests/test_windowhold.py W4: two objects on DIFFERENT z streams hold bitwise-identical beta at every update)."):
        print(ln)

    print("\nS7  THE NOISE FLOOR through corpus_exclusions.filter_rows (OPERATIONS 36), 227.6's definitions")
    if os.path.exists(csvpath):
        floor_section(csvpath)
    else:
        print("  corpus not readable at %s" % csvpath)
    return 0


def floor_section(csvpath):
    sys.path.insert(0, HERE)
    import corpus_exclusions as CE
    sys.path.pop(0)
    naive = [r for r in csv.DictReader(open(csvpath)) if not r.get("run", "").startswith("cvt9-")]
    filt = CE.filter_rows(naive)
    tsv = os.path.join(REPO, "results", "CORPUS-EXCLUSIONS.tsv")
    print("  corpus %s  sha %s  %d rows (cvt9- excluded: none exist), %d after filter_rows; exclusion list sha %s"
          % (csvpath, hashlib.sha256(open(csvpath, "rb").read()).hexdigest(), len(naive), len(filt),
             hashlib.sha256(open(tsv, "rb").read()).hexdigest()))
    out = {}
    for k, net in (("R18ALL", "ResNet18_c100"), ("PLAIN", "PlainNet18_c100")):
        sf, sn = DV4.pooled_sigma(filt, net), DV4.pooled_sigma(naive, net)
        out[k] = sf[0]
        print("  SIGMA_%-7s FILTERED %r (df %d, %d cells)   NAIVE %r (df %d, %d cells)" % (k, sf[0], sf[1], sf[2], sn[0], sn[1], sn[2]))
