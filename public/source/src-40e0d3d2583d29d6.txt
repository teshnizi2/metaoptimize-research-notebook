"""PATCH_VGGBN -- `VGG11_bn` / `VGG11_bn_c100`: a PLAIN (NON-RESIDUAL) BatchNorm
convolutional network, AND NOTHING ELSE CHANGED.  Patches `build_network.py` ONLY.
**NO OPTIMISER CHANGE.  `Optimizers/HF.py` IS NOT TOUCHED BY THIS PATCH.  Neither is
`train.py`, `load_data.py`, `Optimizers/build_optimizer.py` or anything under
`analysis/`.**

=============================================================================
WHY -- THE SCOPE LIMIT, AND THE STRUCTURAL READING THAT MAKES *THIS* NET THE TEST
=============================================================================
Every row of `results/all_runs.csv` is a ResNet.  Re-derived at registration on the
2,761-row corpus, `cvg1-` excluded: eleven distinct `network` values
(ResNet18 1854, ResNet18_c100 518, ResNet34 141, ResNet10 110, ResNet50 67,
resnet18 26, ResNet18_gn 17, ResNet10_c100 9, ResNet18_tin 9, ResNet34_c100 9,
ResNet101 1), and ZERO rows whose `network` does not match `^[Rr]es[Nn]et`.  That is
the campaign's single largest scope limit and it is the objection every granularity
finding shares.

It bites hardest on the tensor-level result.  CORRECTIONS 187 reports that on the
standard CIFAR-100 cell, moving three named 512-parameter layer4 BatchNorm SCALES --
1-based indices 50, 53, 59 -- out of the shared step-size group lifts plateau5 from
23.2807 to 70.2113, the whole in-batch scalar-to-layerwise gap, while the same move
on their layer-2 structural homologues (20, 23, 29) gives -0.0633 pp.

RE-DERIVED HERE ON THE LIVE `ResNet18_c100` (62 tensors, 11,220,132 parameters), the
five 512-wide 1-D `*.weight` tensors -- every 512-wide BatchNorm scale in the whole
model -- are at 47, 50, 53, 56, 59:

    47  layer4.0.bn1.weight          NOT a carrier
    50  layer4.0.bn2.weight          CARRIER
    53  layer4.0.shortcut.1.weight   CARRIER
    56  layer4.1.bn1.weight          NOT a carrier
    59  layer4.1.bn2.weight          CARRIER

Read that against `BasicBlock.forward`, which is, verbatim from the live source:

    out = F.relu(self.bn1(self.conv1(x)))
    out = self.bn2(self.conv2(out))
    out += self.shortcut(x)
    out = F.relu(out)

The three CARRIERS are EXACTLY the 512-wide BN scales whose output is a SUMMAND OF A
RESIDUAL ADDITION -- `bn2` on the residual branch of each of layer4's two blocks, and
`shortcut.1` on the identity branch of the one block that has a projection.  The two
NON-CARRIERS are EXACTLY the two `bn1` scales, which sit mid-branch behind a ReLU and
feed no addition.  The split is 3/2 and it is perfect.

That is a re-derivation from the live manifest plus the live source, not a quotation,
and it is stated here as a HYPOTHESIS ABOUT THE CARRIER SET, not as an established
result: nothing in the corpus has manipulated residual structure, so "the carriers are
the BN scales that feed a residual add" and "the carriers are the deepest, widest BN
scales" are the same measurement seen twice.  ResNet cannot separate them, because on
a ResNet the deepest BN scales ALL feed adds.

    A NET WITH NO RESIDUAL ADDITION SEPARATES THEM.  On a plain conv stack, NO BN
    scale feeds an addition, and the deepest 512-wide BN scales still exist.

=============================================================================
WHAT THIS PATCH MAKES POSSIBLE, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
=============================================================================
IT IS A TEST OF: does the scalar-to-layerwise plateau5 gap -- the gap the whole
    granularity programme is about -- exist AT ALL on a non-residual architecture,
    and does the architecture expose a carrier set at all?

IT IS **NOT** a one-variable ablation of the residual connection.  VGG11_bn differs
from ResNet18 in depth (8 weighted conv layers vs 20), in channel schedule, in
downsampling (max-pool vs strided conv), and in having no projection shortcuts.  It is
a DIFFERENT FAMILY that happens to lack residual additions.  Anyone writing this up as
"the residual connection is the carrier" is over-reading it, and this docstring is part
of that prohibition.  The clean one-variable ablation is a separate, cheaper object --
ResNet18 with `out += self.shortcut(x)` deleted -- and it is NOT built here, on
purpose: it is priced and named as the follow-on in CORRECTIONS 188 so that this patch
adds exactly one testable thing.

WHY VGG11_bn AND NOT THE ALTERNATIVES.
  * A **plain CNN with NO normalisation** removes BatchNorm and residuals at once, and
    an 8-to-11-layer unnormalised conv net under a meta-learned step size bounded above
    by exp(-2.3026) = 0.1 is a serious training-failure risk.  A batch whose arms both
    land at the floor decides nothing, and the campaign forbids predicting an arm at
    the floor.  REJECTED.
  * An **MLP** removes convolutions, BatchNorm and residuals at once, so the finding is
    not even STATABLE on it (no BN scales exist to isolate), and its CIFAR-100 plateau
    is low enough that the scalar arm risks the floor.  REJECTED.
  * A **small ViT** keeps residual additions and swaps BatchNorm for LayerNorm -- a
    genuinely interesting object, but it does NOT test the residual reading, it is the
    largest build of the four, and a from-scratch ViT on CIFAR-100 at 100 epochs is
    weak enough that arm levels are unpredictable.  REJECTED FOR NOW; priced in 188.
  * **VGG11_bn** keeps convolutions, keeps BatchNorm, keeps a 512-wide deepest stage --
    so the three-BN-scale finding is STATABLE and FALSIFIABLE on it -- and removes
    residual additions.  CHOSEN.

=============================================================================
THE MODEL, AND WHY ITS PARAMETER LIST IS SHAPED THE WAY IT IS
=============================================================================
Standard VGG11 convolutional configuration, CIFAR formulation:

    cfg = [64, 'M', 128, 'M', 256, 256, 'M', 512, 512, 'M', 512, 512, 'M']

Eight 3x3 convolutions (`bias=False`), each followed by `nn.BatchNorm2d` and ReLU;
five 2x2 max-pools take 32 -> 16 -> 8 -> 4 -> 2 -> 1, so the final feature map is
1x1x512 and the head is a SINGLE `nn.Linear(512, num_classes)`.

  * `bias=False` on every conv MATCHES ResNet18's convs, so the 1-D tensors of this
    model are EXACTLY the BatchNorm scales and shifts plus `linear.bias` -- the same
    sentence that is true of ResNet18.  That keeps `nodewise` / `nodewise1d` /
    `chunk<K>` meaning on this net what they mean on ResNet18.
  * The single-Linear head MATCHES ResNet18's `self.linear`, and deliberately does NOT
    use torchvision VGG's three-FC classifier, which would add >100M parameters and
    make the head dominate every partition statistic.
  * The sub-modules are registered as `conv1, bn1, conv2, bn2, ..., conv8, bn8, linear`
    by explicit `setattr` in construction order, NOT inside an `nn.Sequential`.  That is
    load-bearing twice over: `named_parameters()` yields readable names that the
    `sets:` grammar can address BY NAME, and it reproduces ResNet18's
    `(conv, bn.weight, bn.bias)` TRIPLE structure, so the identity
    `class == index mod 3` holds at EVERY index from 1 to 24 with zero exceptions --
    the same property CORRECTIONS 161.7c establishes for ResNet18's indices 1..60.

Derived (asserted on the live model by `tests/test_vggbn.py` V2, not trusted here):

    VGG11_bn_c100   26 parameter tensors | 9,274,532 parameters | 17 one-D tensors
                    four 512-wide BN scales, at 1-based indices 14, 17, 20, 23
    VGG11_bn        26 parameter tensors | 9,228,362 parameters (10-way head)

`HF.init_meta` is driven entirely by `[(name, p.data.size()) for name, p in
net.named_parameters()]` and contains NO architecture logic whatsoever; `train.py`
touches the network only through `build_network(args.NN_name, args.device)`;
`analysis/argsline_guard.py` and `bin/_lib_guards.sh` contain no tensor-count
assumption.  So `scalar`, `layerwise`, `nodewise`, `nodewise1d`, `weightwise`,
`chunk<K>`, `permnode<S>`, `[a,b,...]` and `sets:<g>/<g>` all compose on this net with
NO further patch.  `resnet18_blocks` and `resnet50_blocks` remain what they always
were: literal strings whose block lengths sum to 62 and 161, so they raise this file's
own `0/0` on a 26-tensor model -- loudly, which is correct.

=============================================================================
DECLARED CONFOUNDS, WRITTEN BEFORE ANY RUN EXISTS
=============================================================================
  (i)  FAMILY, NOT VARIABLE.  See above: depth, channel schedule and downsampling all
       move with the residual connection.  A null here does NOT license "residuals
       cause the gap"; a positive here does NOT license "residuals are irrelevant".
  (ii) MISTUNING (RULE 11).  This batch runs a NEW network at a cell whose argmax was
       located on ResNet18_c100.  No argmax is located on VGG11_bn by this batch, so a
       SMALL gap is ambiguous between "no gap on this family" and "wrong cell for this
       family", and the scorer's stamp vocabulary says so explicitly.  A LARGE gap is
       clean, because mistuning can only suppress a gap, not manufacture one.
  (iii) FLOOR.  If BOTH arms land near chance the batch is a statement about the
       harness, not about architecture.  The scorer gates on this in advance
       (HARNESS-UNSOUND) and forbids a gap claim under it.

STANDING RULE 20 is observed by the suite: it runs on CPU, and the two new NN-name
strings are single shell tokens of [A-Za-z0-9_] with no `=`, no quote and no leading
`-`.

import os
import sys

P = os.environ.get(
    "BUILD_NET_PATH",
    "/cluster/account_b/metaopt/MetaOptimize/codes/Supervised_tasks/"
    "MetaOptimize/cifar10/build_network.py")

src = open(P).read()

# --- 0. idempotency.  Re-running must not double-patch. ------------------------------
if "PATCH_VGGBN" in src:
    print("PATCH_VGGBN already applied to", P, "-- nothing to do")
    sys.exit(0)

# Cheap sanity line; the real guard is that each anchor occurs EXACTLY ONCE.
if "class ResNet(nn.Module):" not in src or "def build_network(" not in src:
    sys.exit("refusing to patch: %s is not a MetaOptimize build_network.py" % P)

# --- REGION A: the dispatch. ---------------------------------------------------------
# Inserted immediately AFTER the ResNet18_tin dispatch, i.e. still inside
# build_network() and above its terminal `0/0`.  Every pre-existing network_name
# returns before reaching this point, so no existing dispatch changes.
aA = """    if network_name == 'ResNet18_tin':
        return ResNet_tin(BasicBlock, [2, 2, 2, 2], num_classes=200).to(device)
"""
assert src.count(aA) == 1, "anchor A count=%d -- refusing to patch" % src.count(aA)

REGION_A = """
    # --- PATCH_VGGBN: the ONLY new names.  A PLAIN (non-residual) BatchNorm conv
    # stack: no shortcut, no addition, but BatchNorm scales and a 512-wide deepest
    # stage retained, so a carrier-isolation contrast is statable on it.  Reachable
    # only by an exact --NN-name, neither of which any prior batch used. ---
    if network_name == 'VGG11_bn':
        return VGG_bn(VGG_CFG11, num_classes=10).to(device)
    if network_name == 'VGG11_bn_c100':
        return VGG_bn(VGG_CFG11, num_classes=100).to(device)
"""
src = src.replace(aA, aA + REGION_A, 1)

# --- REGION B: the class, appended at END OF FILE. -----------------------------------
# Depends on nothing in the file but `nn` and `F`; nothing in the file depends on it
# except REGION A.
REGION_B = '''

# --- PATCH_VGGBN ----------------------------------------------------------------
# A plain VGG11-with-BatchNorm in the CIFAR formulation.  Present so that the
# campaign has ONE architecture with NO residual addition anywhere, while keeping
# BatchNorm, convolutions and a 512-wide deepest stage -- the three things the
# tensor-level finding is stated in terms of.
#
# Sub-modules are registered by explicit setattr in construction order, NOT inside an
# nn.Sequential, for two reasons that are load-bearing rather than cosmetic:
#   (1) named_parameters() then yields conv1.weight / bn1.weight / bn1.bias / ... ,
#       names the `sets:` grammar (PATCH_NAMESETS) can address BY NAME;
#   (2) it reproduces ResNet18's (conv, bn.weight, bn.bias) TRIPLE structure, so
#       `class == index mod 3` holds at every index 1..24 with zero exceptions.
# Convs carry bias=False, exactly as ResNet18's do, so the 1-D tensors of this model
# are exactly the BatchNorm scales and shifts plus linear.bias.
# Five max-pools take 32 -> 16 -> 8 -> 4 -> 2 -> 1, so the head is a single Linear,
# matching ResNet's self.linear rather than torchvision VGG's three-FC classifier.
VGG_CFG11 = [64, 'M', 128, 'M', 256, 256, 'M', 512, 512, 'M', 512, 512, 'M']


def _vgg_make(cfg):
    """(channel list, pool-after-conv-index set) from a VGG cfg. Pure, no torch."""
    chans, pools, k = [], set(), 0
    for v in cfg:
        if v == 'M':
            if k == 0:
                0/0  # a cfg may not begin with a pool -- fail loudly
            pools.add(k)
        else:
            chans.append(int(v))
            k += 1
    return chans, pools


class VGG_bn(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, cfg, num_classes=10):
        super(VGG_bn, self).__init__()
        chans, pools = _vgg_make(cfg)
        self.n_conv = len(chans)
        self.pools = pools
        in_c = 3
        for i, c in enumerate(chans, 1):
            setattr(self, 'conv%d' % i,
                    nn.Conv2d(in_c, c, kernel_size=3, stride=1, padding=1, bias=False))
            setattr(self, 'bn%d' % i, nn.BatchNorm2d(c))
            in_c = c
        self.linear = nn.Linear(in_c, num_classes)

    def forward(self, x):
        out = x
        for i in range(1, self.n_conv + 1):
            out = F.relu(getattr(self, 'bn%d' % i)(getattr(self, 'conv%d' % i)(out)))
            if i in self.pools:
                out = F.max_pool2d(out, 2)
        out = out.view(out.size(0), -1)
        out = self.linear(out)
        return out
'''
assert src.endswith("        return out\n"), "anchor B: unexpected end of file"
src = src + REGION_B

open(P, "w").write(src)
print("PATCH_VGGBN applied to", P)
print("  REGION A: 2 dispatch names (VGG11_bn, VGG11_bn_c100) after the ResNet18_tin branch")
print("  REGION B: VGG_CFG11, _vgg_make, class VGG_bn appended at end of file")
print("  now run: python3 tests/test_vggbn.py --pre <backup> --post", P)
