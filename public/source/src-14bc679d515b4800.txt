"""cvt8 REGISTRATION-TIME DERIVATIONS (CORRECTIONS 248).  Reads ONLY landed probe records and raw .out files of the
ResNet18_c100 batches at ciso1's cell (ciso1, cdep1, ciso2, cvt7; ctd1 printed out of rule), the corpus through
analysis/corpus_exclusions.filter_rows, and (if present) the PlainNet MUTE records RULE P used at 237.3.  Nothing here
reads a cvt8 run.  What it prints is copied into analysis/cVT8_doseroute_score.py as LITERALS (the replay file's sha256,
P_BIG, the noise floor); that scorer's selftest re-runs derive_knots() on the host's records and requires the replay
file BYTE FOR BYTE.

THE QUESTION (247.4(1)-(2), 247.13 R1).  cvt7 (TRANSFERS-GRADED): on ResNet18_c100 the carrier group {layer4.0.bn2,
layer4.0.shortcut.1, layer4.1.bn2} held on k01's own trajectory (HOLDHIGH, tri:8609, peak -5.21) costs ~20 pp of ISO's
47 pp rescue, not all of it.  Two bounds stop the PlainNet sentence being stated for ResNet: (1) DOSE -- PlainNet's
HOLDHIGH replayed MUTE's trajectory (tri:9428, peak -4.39), ~0.8 log units larger; (2) ROUTE -- HOLDHIGH's free
complement collapsed at epoch ~53 instead of ISO's ~96, so P_HIGH moved both.  cvt8 crosses the carrier group's dose
(HIGH = tri:8609, BIG = tri:9428) with the complement's path (FREE Lion, or FORCED open-loop onto ISO's measured
complement path, ISOPATH), plus LOWISOPATH (group at the floor, complement on ISOPATH) as the complement-hold positive
control.  The complement hold is PATCH_RESTHOLD (patches/patch_resthold.py; ENV REST_HOLD=tri:<P> | rec:<id>),
additive on top of cvt7's PATCH_GROUPHOLD tree.

RULE I (written here, before any cvt8 run) -- WHICH RUNS ARE ELIGIBLE, AND THE REPLAY.
  Candidates: EVERY row of results/all_runs.csv with network ResNet18_c100 whose granularity parses (by name, on the
  62-tensor model) to ISO's partition: two groups, group 1 = {50, 53, 59} = the three carriers.  (Any spelling of the
  same partition is a candidate, not only ISO's registered string.)  The frame is the corpus AT REGISTRATION (sha
  355c0977..., 3,085 rows, highest job id 5022526): rows with a higher job id -- cvt8's own runs and anything landed
  later -- are listed as `LANDED AFTER REGISTRATION` and never enter the replay (REG_MAX_JID).
  A candidate is ELIGIBLE iff ALL of:
    E1  its raw .out exists under <runs> and carries exactly ONE ARGS line with ISO's cell: --optimizer HF --alg-base
        SGDm --momentum-param-base 0.99 --weight-decay-base 0.1 --alg-meta Lion --momentum-param-meta 0.99
        --Lion-beta2-meta 0.9 --weight-decay-meta 0 --dataset CIFAR100 --NN-name ResNet18_c100 --batch-size 100
        --gamma 1 --meta-stepsize 1e-3 --alpha0 1e-6 and --num-epochs >= 100;
    E2  exactly ONE ENV line, == ciso1's byte for byte with PROBE_DIR stripped (AUGMENT=1, BETA_CLIP=-15:-2.3026,
        HIER=none, SCHED=none, PROBE=100);
    E3  exactly ONE `PROBE_TENSOR: on every=100 type=blockwise tensors=62` line;
    E4  NO intervention: it prints no ON line of any kind corpus_exclusions.KINDS knows (VOTE_W / BETA_HOLD /
        GROUP_HOLD / COMP_HOLD -- an `off` line or no line at all is fine) AND it is not listed in
        results/CORPUS-EXCLUSIONS.tsv;
    E5  the CSV row is complete, not superseded;
    E6  its probe.jsonl holds records at steps 0, 100, ..., 49,900 (the first 100 epochs), each with beta[0].
  --num-epochs > 100 is ELIGIBLE (ciso2, 250 epochs): the harness passes num_epochs to NOTHING but train.py's epoch loop
  (HF receives no horizon; SCHED=none; build_optimizer's cosine defaults belong to the AdamW / SGD reference arms, not
  HF), so its first 50,000 updates are the same process as a 100-epoch run's; only records with step < 50,000 are read
  (cvt7's RULE P read ciso2 the same way, 243.3).
  REPLAY: for record index k = 0..499 (step s = 100k, written after meta update n = s + 2):
      v_k = median over the eligible runs (listed in ascending job id) of the record's complement beta[0]
            (python double; with an even count, the mean of the two middle values)
      n_k = 100k + 2
  The file patches/cvt8_isopath.json stores {id, rule, source, n, v} as sorted-key compact JSON.  The patch's schedule
  (PATCH_RESTHOLD rec:, the same arithmetic as PATCH_COMPHOLD's rec:, 242.3(2)):
      n <  n_0            hold(n) = b0
      n_k <= n < n_{k+1}  hold(n) = v_k + (v_{k+1} - v_k) (n - n_k) / float(n_{k+1} - n_k)
      n >= n_last         hold(n) = v_last
      then min(hi, max(lo, .)), stored into float32.
  TOLERANCE, stated and proved in S2: (a) at each of the 500 record steps the applied float32 value is within 1e-6 of
  the measured median; (b) between records it is linear, while every eligible trajectory moves by at most 100 ms = 0.1
  log units (up to float32 accumulation) between two records, so the schedule is within 0.1 log units of the
  piecewise-exact median path at every update.

THE DOSE (S3).  HOLDBIG = tri:9428: the SAME integer, hence BITWISE the same schedule, as PlainNet's HOLDHIGH /
HIGHHEADPATH (cvt4, cvt6; b0, ms and the clamp are identical on both networks), so the network-dose confound of 247.4(1)
is removed in absolute step size.  S3 prints why 9428 and not a larger dose: every ResNet trajectory measured at this
cell on a carrier-sized isolated group (the carriers, ISO's complement, HOLDHIGH's free complement, ciso1's count-matched
CTRL group) peaks at or below P ~ 9500; 9428 sits within 0.02 log units of the median peak of the only isolated
three-tensor group that STALLED at this cell (ciso1 CTRL, P 9447).  The dose matched RELATIVE to each network's own k01
peak would be 8609 + (9428 - 8437) = 9600 -- 0.172 log units above 9428, printed as the alternative, not chosen.

Sections:
  S0  RULE I eligibility: every candidate, every clause, the eligible list
  S1  ISO's complement beta[0] is NOT a max-rate triangle (why a replay file)
  S2  RULE I -> the replay file; tolerance (a), (b); fidelity in-sample and out-of-sample (cvt7 HOLDLOW / HOLDISO
      complements: carriers held small, complement free)
  S3  the dose: RULE P re-run (8609, 5153) and P_BIG 9428; the schedules; measured ResNet trajectories at this cell
  S4  G-BITE non-vacuity on landed records: each forced arm's BROKEN-REST_HOLD null is a landed FREE complement; the
      carrier hold tri:9428 against landed unheld / differently held carrier groups
  S5  descriptive: ISOPATH against HOLDHIGH's free complement (the contrast HIGHISOPATH makes) and against k01's shared
  S6  the noise floor on the current corpus (227.6's definitions) through corpus_exclusions.filter_rows

  python3 analysis/cvt8_registration_derivations.py <runs dir holding ciso1/ cdep1/ ciso2/ cvt7/ and the .out files>
          [<all_runs.csv>] [--write <patches/cvt8_isopath.json>]

STEPS_PER_EPOCH = 500

ISOSPEC = "sets:1-49,51-52,54-58,60-62/layer4.0.bn2.weight,layer4.0.shortcut.1.weight,layer4.1.bn2.weight"
CARRIER_IDX = (50, 53, 59)
P_HIGH = 8609                   # 243.3 RULE P (k01's shared beta at this cell)
P_ISO = 5153                    # 243.3 RULE P (ISO's carrier group)
P_BIG = 9428                    # 237.3 RULE P on PlainNet MUTE's shared beta = cvt4 / cvt6 HOLDHIGH

ENV_ISO = ("ENV: AUGMENT=1 BETA_CLIP=-15:-2.3026 HIER=none LAM=na ETA_RATIO=na COS_TOTAL=default COS_WARMUP=default "
           "SCHED=none SCHED_TOTAL=none SCHED_WARMUP=none SCHED_MIN=none PROBE=100 EB_RHO=na EB_LOG=0")
ARGS_CELL = {"optimizer": "HF", "alg-base": "SGDm", "momentum-param-base": "0.99", "weight-decay-base": "0.1",
             "alg-meta": "Lion", "momentum-param-meta": "0.99", "Lion-beta2-meta": "0.9", "weight-decay-meta": "0",

PT_LINE = re.compile(r"^PROBE_TENSOR: on every=100 type=blockwise tensors=62 ")


def r18_names():
    t = ["conv1.weight", "bn1.weight", "bn1.bias"]
    inp = 64
    for li, w in enumerate((64, 128, 256, 512), 1):
        for b in (0, 1):
            stride = 2 if (li > 1 and b == 0) else 1
            cin = inp if b == 0 else w
            pre = "layer%d.%d." % (li, b)
            t += [pre + "conv1.weight", pre + "bn1.weight", pre + "bn1.bias", pre + "conv2.weight", pre + "bn2.weight",
                  pre + "bn2.bias"]
            if stride != 1 or cin != w:
                t += [pre + "shortcut.0.weight", pre + "shortcut.1.weight", pre + "shortcut.1.bias"]
        inp = w
    return t + ["linear.weight", "linear.bias"]


NAMES = r18_names()


def parse_sets(spec):
    if not spec.startswith("sets:"):
        return None
    out = []
    for grp in spec[5:].split("/"):
        idx = []
        for tok in grp.split(","):
            if re.match(r"^\d+-\d+$", tok):
                a, b = (int(x) for x in tok.split("-"))
                idx += list(range(a, b + 1))
            elif re.match(r"^\d+$", tok):
                idx.append(int(tok))
            elif tok in NAMES:
                idx.append(NAMES.index(tok) + 1)
            else:
                return None
        out.append(sorted(idx))
    return out


def is_iso_partition(gran):
    g = parse_sets(gran or "")
    return (g is not None and len(g) == 2 and g[1] == list(CARRIER_IDX)
            and sorted(g[0] + g[1]) == list(range(1, len(NAMES) + 1)))


def parse_args_line(line):
    toks = line[len("ARGS:"):].split()
    flags, cur = {}, None
    for t in toks:
        if t.startswith("--"):
            cur = t[2:]
            flags.setdefault(cur, []).append(None)
        elif cur is not None:
            v = flags[cur][-1]
            flags[cur][-1] = t if v is None else v + " " + t
    return flags


def find_out(runs, run, jid):
    for p in (os.path.join(runs, "%s-%s.out" % (run, jid)), os.path.join(runs, run.split("-")[0], "%s-%s.out" % (run, jid))):
        if os.path.exists(p):
            return p
    return None


def probe_path(runs, run):
    b = run.split("-")[0]
    return os.path.join(runs, b, "probe_%s" % run, "probe.jsonl")


def load_first100(runs, run):
    p = probe_path(runs, run)
    if not os.path.exists(p):
        return None
    out = []
    for ln in open(p):
        if not ln.strip():
            continue
        r = json.loads(ln)
        if r["step"] >= N_RECORDS * 100:
            break
        out.append(r)
    return out


_ELIG = {}


def eligibility(runs, csvpath):
    """RULE I, S0.  -> (eligible run names in ascending job id, [(run, jid, epochs, verdict, reasons)] by job id)"""
    key = (os.path.abspath(runs), os.path.abspath(csvpath))
    if key not in _ELIG:
        _ELIG[key] = _eligibility(runs, csvpath)
    return _ELIG[key]


def _eligibility(runs, csvpath):
    listed = CE.keys()
    rows = [r for r in csv.DictReader(open(csvpath)) if r.get("network") == NET and is_iso_partition(r.get("granularity"))]
    table, elig = [], []
    for r in rows:
        run, jid = r["run"], r["job_id"]
        why = []
        if int(jid) > REG_MAX_JID:
            table.append((run, jid, r.get("epochs_requested"), "NOT ELIGIBLE", ["LANDED AFTER REGISTRATION (job id > %d)" % REG_MAX_JID]))
            continue
        p = find_out(runs, run, jid)
        if p is None:
            why.append("E1 no raw .out")
        else:
            lines = open(p, errors="replace").read().splitlines()
            args = [ln for ln in lines if ln.startswith("ARGS:")]
            if len(args) != 1:
                why.append("E1 %d ARGS lines" % len(args))
            else:
                f = parse_args_line(args[0])
                bad = [k for k, v in ARGS_CELL.items() if f.get(k, [None])[-1] != v]
                ne = f.get("num-epochs", [None])[-1]
                if bad or any(len(v) > 1 for v in f.values()) or not (ne and ne.isdigit() and int(ne) >= 100):
                    why.append("E1 ARGS not ISO's cell (%s, num-epochs %s)" % (bad, ne))
            envs = [re.sub(r" PROBE_DIR=\S*", "", ln) for ln in lines if ln.startswith("ENV:")]
            if envs != [ENV_ISO]:
                why.append("E2 ENV %r" % (envs[:1],))
            if len([ln for ln in lines if PT_LINE.match(ln)]) != 1 or len([ln for ln in lines if ln.startswith("PROBE_TENSOR:")]) != 1:
                why.append("E3 PROBE_TENSOR line")
            on = [k for k, off in CE.KINDS if any(ln.startswith(k) and ln != off for ln in lines)]
            if on:
                why.append("E4 intervention ON: %s" % "/".join(on))
        if (run, jid) in listed:
            why.append("E4 listed in CORPUS-EXCLUSIONS.tsv")
        if not (r.get("complete") == "1" and r.get("superseded") == "0"):
            why.append("E5 complete %s superseded %s" % (r.get("complete"), r.get("superseded")))
        recs = load_first100(runs, run)
        if recs is None or [x["step"] for x in recs] != [100 * k for k in range(N_RECORDS)]:
            why.append("E6 probe records not steps 0..49900")
        table.append((run, jid, r.get("epochs_requested"), "ELIGIBLE" if not why else "NOT ELIGIBLE", why))
        if not why:
            elig.append((int(jid), run))
    table.sort(key=lambda t: int(t[1]))
    return [run for _j, run in sorted(elig)], table


def source_runs(runs, names, g=0):
    return [(nm, [(r["step"], float(r["beta"][g])) for r in load_first100(runs, nm)]) for nm in names]


RULE_TEXT = ("RULE I (CORRECTIONS 248): v_k = median over EVERY eligible landed ISO run at ciso1's cell (ResNet18_c100; "
             "ISO's partition; ISO's ARGS cell, --num-epochs >= 100, records with step < 50000; ciso1's ENV line; "
             "PROBE_TENSOR blockwise 62; no intervention ON line, not in CORPUS-EXCLUSIONS.tsv; complete) of probe record "
             "k's complement beta[0]; n_k = 100k + 2")


def derive_knots(runs, csvpath):
    """RULE I.  -> (n list, v list, source names)"""
    elig, _t = eligibility(runs, csvpath)
    src = source_runs(runs, elig, 0)
    n = [100 * k + 2 for k in range(N_RECORDS)]
    v = [DV6.median([tr[k][1] for _nm, tr in src]) for k in range(N_RECORDS)]
    return n, v, [nm for nm, _tr in src]


def knots_json(n, v, names):
    return json.dumps({"id": FILE_ID, "rule": RULE_TEXT, "source": names, "n": n, "v": v},
                      sort_keys=True, separators=(",", ":")) + "\n"


def test_json():
    """TEST-ONLY replay (never a batch arm): the natural rise to update 102, then down to the clamp by update 302."""
    return json.dumps({"id": TEST_ID, "rule": "TEST ONLY (CORRECTIONS 248): never used by a cvt8 arm",
                       "source": [], "n": [2, 102, 302], "v": [f32(B0 + MS), f32(B0 + 101 * MS), LO]},
                      sort_keys=True, separators=(",", ":")) + "\n"


def sched_rec(kn, kv):
    return [(100 * k, DV6.rec_value(100 * k + 2, kn, kv)) for k in range(N_RECORDS)]


def sched_tri(P):
    return [(100 * k, DV4.v_of(100 * k + 2, P)) for k in range(N_RECORDS)]


def clamp_update(P):
    n = P + 1
    while DV4.v_of(n, P) != LO:
        n += 1
    return n


def floor(csvpath):
    naive = [r for r in csv.DictReader(open(csvpath)) if not r.get("run", "").startswith("cvt8-")]
    filt = CE.filter_rows(naive)
    return {"rows": len(naive), "rows_filtered": len(filt),
            "R18ALL": DV4.pooled_sigma(filt, NET), "PLAIN": DV4.pooled_sigma(filt, "PlainNet18_c100"),
            "R18ALL_naive": DV4.pooled_sigma(naive, NET), "PLAIN_naive": DV4.pooled_sigma(naive, "PlainNet18_c100"),
            "tsv_sha": hashlib.sha256(open(CE.DEFAULT_TSV, "rb").read()).hexdigest()}


def main():
    argv = list(sys.argv[1:])
    write = None
    if "--write" in argv:
        i = argv.index("--write")
        write = argv[i + 1]
        del argv[i:i + 2]
    runs = argv[0] if argv else os.path.join(os.path.dirname(REPO), "runs_cluster_b")
    csvpath = argv[1] if len(argv) > 1 else os.path.join(REPO, "results", "all_runs.csv")
    print("cvt8 registration derivations (CORRECTIONS 248)")
    print("  runs %s" % runs)
    print("  corpus %s sha %s" % (csvpath, hashlib.sha256(open(csvpath, "rb").read()).hexdigest()))
    print("  b0 = float32(log 1e-6) = %r ; ms = %r ; clamp [%r, %r] ; BH_TOL %r" % (B0, MS, LO, HI, BH_TOL))

    print("\nS0  RULE I ELIGIBILITY: every CSV row of %s whose granularity parses to ISO's partition (group 1 = %s)" % (NET, CARRIER_IDX))
    elig, table = eligibility(runs, csvpath)
    for run, jid, ep, verdict, why in table:
        print("  %-22s %-8s epochs %-4s %-13s %s" % (run, jid, ep, verdict, "; ".join(why) if why else "E1-E6 all hold"))
    print("  candidates %d; ELIGIBLE %d: %s" % (len(table), len(elig), " ".join(elig)))

    src = source_runs(runs, elig, 0)
    print("\nS1  ISO's COMPLEMENT beta[0] IS NOT A MAX-RATE TRIANGLE (%d eligible runs)" % len(src))
    worst_tri = 1e9
    for nm, tr in src:
        s_pk, b_pk = DV4.peak(tr)
        bt, bP = DV4.min_over_P_maxdev(tr)
        worst_tri = min(worst_tri, bt)
        print("  %-20s peak %.4f @ %5.1f  @20 %.3f @30 %.3f @40 %.3f @50 %.3f @60 %.3f @80 %.3f  r<=2 from %5.1f  last %.3f  best triangle max|dev| %.3f (P %d)"
              % (nm, b_pk, s_pk / 500.0, DV6.at(tr, 20), DV6.at(tr, 30), DV6.at(tr, 40), DV6.at(tr, 50), DV6.at(tr, 60),
                 DV6.at(tr, 80), DV6.mag_pin_epoch(tr), tr[-1][1], bt, bP))
    print("  => the BEST max-rate triangle misses EVERY eligible run by >= %.3f log units somewhere; RULE I replays the measured"
          " median instead (243.3(1) found 7.29-7.40 against the arm's OWN triangle)." % worst_tri)

    print("\nS2  RULE I -> the replay file")
    kn, kv, names = derive_knots(runs, csvpath)
    body = knots_json(kn, kv, names)
    sha = hashlib.sha256(body.encode()).hexdigest()
    print("  source (%d runs): %s" % (len(names), " ".join(names)))
    print("  knots %d, n %d..%d every 100; v_0 %r (== float32(b0 + ms) %r: %s); v_last %r; max v %r at knot %d (epoch %.1f)"
          % (len(kn), kn[0], kn[-1], kv[0], f32(B0 + MS), kv[0] == f32(B0 + MS), kv[-1], max(kv), kv.index(max(kv)),
             (kn[kv.index(max(kv))] - 2) / 500.0))
    print("  REPLAY FILE %s.json  %d bytes  sha256 %s" % (FILE_ID, len(body), sha))
    tbody = test_json()
    print("  TEST-ONLY FILE %s.json  %d bytes  sha256 %s" % (TEST_ID, len(tbody), hashlib.sha256(tbody.encode()).hexdigest()))
    sched = sched_rec(kn, kv)
    at_knot = max(abs(v - kv[k]) for k, (_s, v) in enumerate(sched))
    print("  TOLERANCE (a): at the 500 record steps max |float32 schedule - measured median| = %.3e (<= 1e-6: %s)"
          % (at_knot, at_knot <= 1e-6))
    jump = max(abs(tr[k + 1][1] - tr[k][1]) for _nm, tr in src for k in range(N_RECORDS - 1))
    kjump = max(abs(kv[k + 1] - kv[k]) for k in range(N_RECORDS - 1))
    print("  TOLERANCE (b): largest move of ANY eligible trajectory between two records %.7f (<= 0.1 + 1e-4: %s); of the median %.7f"
          % (jump, jump <= 0.1 + 1e-4, kjump))
    print("  => the schedule equals the measured median at every record step (<= 1e-6) and is within 0.1 log units of the"
          " piecewise-exact median path at every update.")
    lin_ok = all(DV6.rec_value(n, kn, kv) == f32(min(HI, max(LO, kv[(n - 2) // 100] + (kv[(n - 2) // 100 + 1] - kv[(n - 2) // 100]) * ((n - 2) % 100) / 100.0)))
                 for n in range(2, kn[-1], 37))
    print("  (the bisection form == the uniform-knot form at every 37th update: %s)" % lin_ok)
    rs = (DV6.r_window(sched, 15, 30), DV6.r_window(sched, 30, 60), DV6.r_window(sched, 60, 100))
    print("  FIDELITY, IN-SAMPLE: max|rec - sched| / mean|.| / records > BH_TOL ; r(15-30) r(30-60) r(60-100) ; r<=2 from")
    print("    %-22s %28s  r %8.1f %8.1f %6.2f   r<=2 from %s" % ("SCHEDULE", "", rs[0], rs[1], rs[2], DV6.mag_pin_epoch(sched)))
    for nm, tr in src:
        mx, mn, nb = DV6.dev(tr, sched)
        print("    %-22s %.4f / %.4f / %3d   r %8.1f %8.1f %6.2f   r<=2 from %.1f" % (nm, mx, mn, nb, DV6.r_window(tr, 15, 30),
              DV6.r_window(tr, 30, 60), DV6.r_window(tr, 60, 100), DV6.mag_pin_epoch(tr)))
    print("  FIDELITY, OUT-OF-SAMPLE (not in the median; carriers held small, complement FREE): cvt7 HOLDLOW / HOLDISO")
    for arm in ("HOLDLOW", "HOLDISO"):
        for s in (99, 100, 101):
            nm = "cvt7-%s-s%d" % (arm, s)
            tr = source_runs(runs, [nm], 0)[0][1]
            mx, mn, nb = DV6.dev(tr, sched)
            print("    %-22s %.4f / %.4f / %3d   r %8.1f %8.1f %6.2f   r<=2 from %s" % (nm, mx, mn, nb, DV6.r_window(tr, 15, 30),
                  DV6.r_window(tr, 30, 60), DV6.r_window(tr, 60, 100), DV6.mag_pin_epoch(tr)))
