--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0.  WHAT IS AT RISK, AND WHY THESE TWO CELLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
docs/MASTER-TABLE.md row 24 reads, in its "so what" column:

    "partitioning buys TOLERANCE to an over-large meta-stepsize, not peak
     accuracy; the granularity claim is a robustness claim"

and its evidence is the falloff ABOVE each arm's meta-stepsize optimum
(FINDINGS 57.2; CORRECTIONS 86.3).  CORRECTIONS 148's corpus-wide clamp census
named row 24 as the one finding that RESCOPING DOES NOT SAVE, for a structural
reason: the region the row is about -- ms above the joint optimum ms=1e-4 -- is
the CLAMPED region for partitioned arms and the INTERIOR region for the scalar
arm.  148.5 measured, at ms=1e-3 and the canonical box, scalar 0/33 binding
corpus-wide against 100% for every layerwise / nodewise / weightwise rung on
R10, R18 and ResNet18_c100.  So

    "the fine arm degrades more slowly above its peak"          and
    "the fine arm's runaway groups are caught by a wall the scalar never reaches"

are the SAME OBSERVATION until something separates them.  Restating row 24 as
"in the clamped regime" does not save it, because the regime IS the finding.

Row 24 has no clamp-free replicate.  Of the box ladders the campaign ran
(wc5, cl5, uc5, uc6, bl5, br6, bo6, bo7, bd7, bf8, bf9, ns5, sp8) every one ran
{layerwise, nodewise, weightwise} only -- the SCALAR axis, which is the axis row
24 is about, is the axis the box ladders never varied.

TWO box-released cells DO contain a scalar arm and are complete on disk:

    tc1   R18 / CIFAR-10, AdamW base + Adam meta, ms=1e-3, 100 ep, AUGMENT=1,
          BETA_CLIP=-30:6.0, arms {scalar, resnet18_blocks, layerwise}
          x alpha0 {1e-3, 1e-4} x seeds {0,1}                        (12 rows)
    ub9   R18 / CIFAR-10, AdamW base + Adam meta, ms=1e-3, 100 ep, AUGMENT=0,
          alpha0=1e-6, BETA_CLIP=-60:6.0, same three arms x seeds {0,1,2}
                                                                      (9 rows)

Neither has ever been read as a box-vs-box contrast.  ub9 was scored by the

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  THE ESTIMAND, STATED BEFORE ANY ARM MEAN IS COMPUTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRIMARY METRIC is plateau5 = mean of the LAST 5 test epochs, read from each
run's OWN .out file (train.py prints epochs 0-indexed, so the window is epochs
95..99 at a 100-epoch budget).  The CSV `plateau` column is BANNED as primary and
this scorer never reads it.  `best_test` is not a plateau and is never a primary.
train5 = mean of the last 5 TRAIN epochs is reported alongside EVERY arm.

Row 24's content, at ms=1e-3 (one decade ABOVE the joint optimum ms=1e-4 that
57.2 established for both scalar and layerwise), is that the PARTITIONED arms sit
ABOVE the scalar arm.  So the quantity that carries row 24 is the ARM GAP, not
any arm's absolute level:

    GAP(arm, batch) = mean plateau5 of `arm` in that batch
                    - mean plateau5 of `scalar` in the SAME batch

GAP is always computed WITHIN a batch, because BATCH is the unit of replication
(docs/CLOSEOUT.md:142, docs/MAIN-IDEA-LEDGER.md:20: BATCH F(62,85)=5.47,
p=6.9e-13; SEED F(60,85)=1.21, p=0.213 -- seed is statistically NULL).

The estimand is the CHANGE IN THAT GAP WHEN THE BOX IS RELEASED:

    D_GAP(arm) = GAP(arm, released cell) - mean over canonical batches b of
                 GAP(arm, b)

Averaging already-differenced within-batch contrasts across batches is the
meta-analysis that STANDING RULE 14 explicitly does NOT penalise
(docs/PLAN.md:104).  Differencing two such contrasts across batches, which is
what D_GAP is, cancels the batch MAIN effect but NOT the batch x arm
interaction, and the batch x arm interaction is therefore re-derived from the
corpus below and carried as a floor that does not shrink with n.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.  THE MATCHED CANONICAL CELLS, NAMED IN ADVANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A canonical match must agree with its released cell on network, dataset, base
algorithm, meta algorithm, meta-stepsize, alpha0, epochs, batch size, gamma,
augment and HIER, and differ ONLY in BETA_CLIP (canonical = -15:-2.3026).  The
selection rule, applied by the scorer itself and not by hand:

  (a) corpus rows with complete=1, superseded=0, collapsed=0, epochs_done=100;
  (b) network=ResNet18, dataset=CIFAR10, base=AdamW, meta=Adam,
      meta_stepsize=1e-3, batch_size=100, gamma=1, hier blank;
  (c) alpha0 and augment equal to the released cell's;
  (d) beta_clip = -15:-2.3026;
  (e) granularity in {scalar, resnet18_blocks, layerwise};
  (f) THE BATCH MUST CARRY ALL THREE ARMS.  A within-batch arm gap needs the
      scalar anchor in the SAME batch; a batch without one cannot enter.
      Batches dropped by (f) are NAMED in the output, not silently discarded.

By inspection of the corpus at registration time this rule admits:
    alpha0=1e-3, AUGMENT=1  ->  batches `a0` and `I1`     (3 arms x 3 seeds each)
    alpha0=1e-4, AUGMENT=1  ->  batch  `a0`               (3 arms x 3 seeds)
    alpha0=1e-6, AUGMENT=0  ->  batches `PP` and `pp`     (3 arms x 3 seeds each)
and drops, by (f), `dc` (layerwise only; `dc-aw-*` additionally carries
HIER=additive and is already excluded by (b)), `i3b` (resnet18_blocks only) and
`I1_inert` (a single layerwise run at alpha0=1e-4).  The scorer RE-DERIVES this
selection; the list here is a statement of what was expected, not an input.

NO COMPARISON IS REFUSED FOR WANT OF A MATCH -- all three released cells have
one.  Had a match been absent the comparison would have been DROPPED and named,
not run against a loosened match.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.  THE NOISE FLOOR, RE-DERIVED BY THIS SCORER FROM THE CORPUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Nothing here is quoted from prose.  Two components:

  sigma_w  -- SEED noise.  Pooled within-(batch x granularity x alpha0 x augment
              x beta_clip) SD of plateau5 over the MATCHED FAMILY: every
              complete, unsuperseded, uncollapsed 100-epoch row with
              R18 / CIFAR10 / AdamW / Adam / ms=1e-3 / batch 100 / gamma 1 /
              HIER blank and granularity in the three arms, at ANY box and ANY
              alpha0.  Cells with n>=2 contribute.  df is printed.

  sigma_BA -- BATCH x ARM noise, the floor on D_GAP.  For each
              (alpha0, augment, non-scalar arm) at the CANONICAL box where >=2
              distinct batches each carry that arm AND scalar, form the
              within-batch GAP per batch and take the deviations of those gaps
              about their own group mean; pool the squared deviations over all
              such groups.  This is the between-batch noise ON THE ESTIMAND.
              A NARROW estimate (the matched family above) and a BROAD estimate
              (the same construction over the WHOLE corpus at the canonical box,
              any network / dataset / ms / base / meta) are both computed and
              the scorer uses the LARGER, which is the conservative choice.
              Registered in advance so the wider one cannot be chosen after the
              fact.

  BARS, all quoted in SE units by --selftest and by the report:

    SE_GAP(cell)  = sigma_w * sqrt(1/n_arm + 1/n_scalar)          [within batch]
    SE_seed(D)    = sigma_w * sqrt( (1/n_a_rel + 1/n_s_rel)
                                    + (1/B^2) * sum_b (1/n_a_b + 1/n_s_b) )
    SE_batch(D)   = sigma_BA * sqrt(1 + 1/B)     [B = # canonical batches; the
                                                  released side is ONE batch and
                                                  this term does not shrink with
                                                  seeds]
    SE_D          = sqrt( SE_seed(D)^2 + SE_batch(D)^2 )

    RESOLVE_GAP   = 2 * SE_GAP     (2 SE)   -- is a gap resolvable at all
    RESOLVE_D     = 2 * SE_D       (2 SE)   -- is a CHANGE in the gap resolvable
    NOISY_BAR     = 3 * sigma_w    (3 sigma_w) -- within-cell SD above this VOIDS
    DEAD_BAR      = 20.00 pp                -- CIFAR-10 chance is 10.00 pp; a run
                                               at or below 20.00 did not train
    MDE           = 2.8 * SE_D     (2.8 SE) -- ~80% power, two-sided alpha 0.05,
                                               printed for every contrast

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.  GATES.  THEY FIRE IN ORDER AND THE FIRST FAILURE DECIDES.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
G0  PROVENANCE.  Every released row must be present in the corpus with
    complete=1, superseded=0, epochs_done=100; its OWN ARGS line must carry no
    repeated flag and must agree with the registered design on --alg-base,
    --alg-meta, --meta-stepsize, --alpha0, --stepsize-groups, --num-epochs,
    --NN-name, --dataset, --batch-size, --gamma; its OWN ENV line must carry the
    registered BETA_CLIP, AUGMENT and HIER=none.  plateau5 re-derived from the
    .out must agree with the CSV column to 0.01 pp.  Any failure -> VOID.
    Fails CLOSED to VOID-PROVENANCE-UNREADABLE if the .out files cannot be read.

G1  RELEASE / MANIPULATION CHECK.  Every canonical-match row's own ENV line must
    read BETA_CLIP=-15:-2.3026 and every released row its own released walls.
    A mismatch between the CSV column and the run's own ENV line -> VOID
    (STANDING RULE 20: a batch's science is what the run's own line says).

G2  OCCUPANCY GATE, AT THE COORDINATE DENOMINATOR (FINDINGS 51.1 settled that
    denominator; per-tensor beta[] readings are the wrong resolution and are not
    used).  For every released run, a FULL streaming pass over every record of
    its own probe.jsonl -- not a sample, which is strictly stronger than
    CORRECTIONS 148.3's 11-record sample.  A run BINDS if any record has
    n_at_lo > 0 or n_at_hi > 0 where the schema carries those fields, else if
    beta_true_min or beta_true_max equals the corresponding wall.

    THE FLOAT32 TRAP, recorded by CORRECTIONS 148.3 and honoured here: the clamp
    writes float32(wall), and float32(-2.3026) = -2.3025999069213867 is NOT the
    Python float -2.3026 parsed from the BETA_CLIP string.  Exact equality is a
    clean FLOOR detector and a BROKEN CEILING detector; it read the corpus
    ceiling as 2.8% when the truth was 9.0%.  This scorer compares against
    float32(wall) with abs tolerance 1e-4 nats.

    A contrast is VOIDED if, in its released cell, the arm-to-arm spread in
    measured binding rate is >= OCC_VOID_SPREAD = 0.50, or any single arm's rate
    is >= OCC_VOID_LEVEL = 0.50 (the release did not release).  A spread >=
    OCC_FLAG_SPREAD = 0.25 is FLAGGED and printed beside every number from that
    contrast but does not void.  If probes cannot be read at all the contrast
    fails CLOSED to UNRESOLVED-OCCUPANCY-UNCHECKED -- never to "clean".

    G2-ASYMMETRY, REGISTERED IN ADVANCE AS A KNOWN LIMITATION AND NOT A GATE:
    the canonical batches `a0`, `I1`, `PP` and `pp` carry NO PROBE directory
    (their ENV lines have no PROBE / PROBE_DIR field at all), so canonical-side
    occupancy is UNMEASURABLE in every one of these three comparisons.  It
    cannot void anything, and it is printed with every contrast.  What is known
    about the canonical side is corpus-level, not cell-level: CORRECTIONS 148.5
    measured 0/33 scalar binding and 100% layerwise binding at ms=1e-3.

G3  DEAD.  Any released or canonical run with plateau5 <= DEAD_BAR ->
    UNRESOLVED-DIVERGED for that contrast.  Registered in advance precisely so
    that "removing the floor stopped it training" can never be scored as "the
    gap collapsed".

G4  NOISY.  Any contributing cell whose within-cell SD exceeds 3 * sigma_w ->
    UNRESOLVED-NOISY for that contrast.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.  THE THREE BRANCHES FOR ROW 24.  ALL THREE ARE REGISTERED; NOT ONLY THE
    INTERESTING ONE.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Per contrast (a released cell x a non-scalar arm), one of:

  SURVIVES   GAP_released > 0 by at least RESOLVE_GAP, and |D_GAP| < RESOLVE_D.
             The partitioned-over-scalar advantage at ms=1e-3 is present with
             the floor 15 or 45 nats further away, so on this cell it is not
             manufactured by the -15 wall.
  REDUCED    GAP_released > 0 by at least RESOLVE_GAP, and D_GAP < -RESOLVE_D.
             Present but smaller with the box released: part of the advantage
             tracked the wall.
  COLLAPSED  D_GAP < -RESOLVE_D and GAP_released is <= 0 or not resolvable.
             Releasing the box removes the advantage.
  INFLATED   D_GAP > +RESOLVE_D.  The advantage is LARGER with the box released,
             which the clamp-artefact account does not predict.
  UNRESOLVED |D_GAP| < RESOLVE_D and GAP_released not resolvable, or any gate
             fired.

Rolled up to row 24, over the SIX contrasts (3 released cells x 2 non-scalar
arms):

  BRANCH A -- ROW 24 STANDS ON THESE CELLS.  >= 4 of 6 contrasts SURVIVE or
      INFLATE, none COLLAPSES.  What it means: the row's robustness claim has a
      clamp-free replicate at ms=1e-3 and its mechanism is not the -15 floor.
      What it does NOT mean: row 24's own numbers (5.871 / 1.060 / 1.801 / 2.593
      pp per decade) are a falloff SLOPE over a whole ms ladder in an SGDm+Lion
      pool; these cells are ONE ms, AdamW+Adam, and re-slope nothing.

  BRANCH B -- ROW 24 MUST BE REWRITTEN.  >= 4 of 6 contrasts COLLAPSE or REDUCE,
      with at least two COLLAPSED.  What it means: with the wall moved away the
      partitioned-over-scalar advantage at an over-large ms does not reproduce,
      so the row's sentence cannot be stated without the clamp in it.

  BRANCH C -- OPEN.  Anything else: mixed outcomes, any VOID, any UNRESOLVED
      majority.  A null is a valid deliverable and this branch is not a failure
      of the analysis.

TWO MODIFIERS ATTACH TO WHATEVER BRANCH FIRES.  They are verdict branches, not
footnotes, and the scorer prints them in the verdict line itself:

  NULL-AMBIGUOUS-TWO-WALL.  Fires on any contrast whose |D_GAP| < RESOLVE_D.
      NEITHER released cell is a one-factor manipulation.  tc1's -30:6.0 moves
      the floor 15.0000 nats down AND the ceiling 8.3026 nats up relative to the
      canonical -15:-2.3026; ub9's -60:6.0 moves the floor 45.0000 nats down AND
      the ceiling 8.3026 nats up.  A null in either is AMBIGUOUS between "the box
      does not matter" and "the two changes cancel", and no amount of n fixes
      that -- only a one-factor released cell would.  (The task brief flagged
      this for tc1; the scorer re-derives the walls and finds it true of ub9 as
      well.)

  CROSS-BATCH-WEAKNESS.  Printed on EVERY line that carries a number.  BATCH is
      the unit of replication (F(62,85)=5.47, p=6.9e-13) and every D_GAP here is
      a CROSS-BATCH comparison.  This is the design's central weakness.  It is
      worse than usual for the alpha0=1e-6 contrast, which is additionally
      CROSS-ACCOUNT: ub9 ran on cluster_b (account_b) and its matched `PP`/`pp`
      batches ran on cluster_a (account_a), so cluster partition and GPU class
      are confounded with the box there.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.  POWER, AND WHY THIS SCORER CANNOT PRINT A SETTLEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
tc1 is n=2 per (arm, alpha0).  Against the campaign's own batch floor
(sd_batch ~ 0.21 pp, docs/PLAN.md:104 -- re-derived here as sigma_BA rather than
quoted) that is UNDERPOWERED, and tc1 is registered as a DIRECTION CHECK, not a
settlement.

The scorer REFUSES to print a settlement verdict.  A settlement would require
BOTH of:
    (i)  every contributing cell at n >= 3, and
    (ii) a ONE-FACTOR box manipulation.
Condition (ii) is FALSE for both released cells by arithmetic on their own ENV
lines -- both move both walls -- so NO contrast this scorer can construct is
ever eligible.  The scorer asserts this in --selftest.  Every verdict token it
emits is prefixed DIRECTION-, and the string "SETTLED" appears in its output
only inside the sentence saying it cannot be printed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.  THE REGISTERING AGENT'S PRIOR, ON RECORD BEFORE THE ARM MEANS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DISCLOSURE, because RULE 21 is about ordering and the ordering here is not
perfectly blind.  Verifying provenance required listing the 21 released rows out
of results/all_runs.csv, so the registering agent HAS SEEN the individual
plateau5 values of tc1 and ub9.  It has NOT computed any arm mean, any gap, any
D_GAP, any noise floor, or any occupancy rate.  What the pre-registration buys
here is therefore that the GATES, the BARS, the MATCHED CELLS and the BRANCHES
are frozen before the contrast exists -- not that the sign is unknown.  Stating
this is worth more than pretending otherwise.

Given that partial sight, the prior on record: MIXED.  The alpha0=1e-3 released
rows put scalar at the TOP, so the registering agent expects COLLAPSED there;
the alpha0=1e-6 released rows put scalar at the BOTTOM, so it expects SURVIVES
there.  A mixed outcome lands in BRANCH C -- OPEN -- which is the branch that
settles nothing, and the agent expects that to be the honest result.

def f32(x):
    """The value float32 actually stores -- see the CORRECTIONS 148.3 trap."""
    return struct.unpack("f", struct.pack("f", float(x)))[0]


def mean(v):
    return statistics.mean(v) if v else float("nan")


def sd(v):
    return statistics.stdev(v) if len(v) >= 2 else float("nan")


def parse_clip(s):
    lo, hi = str(s).split(":")
    return float(lo), float(hi)


def fmt(x, nd=4):
    try:
        if x is None or (isinstance(x, float) and not math.isfinite(x)):
            return "  n/a"
        return ("%%.%df" % nd) % x
    except (TypeError, ValueError):
        return "  n/a"


# =============================================================================
# the corpus
# =============================================================================
def csv_rows(path):
    with open(path) as fh:
        rows = list(_csv.DictReader(fh))
    return [r for r in rows
            if (r.get("superseded") or "0") != "1" and (r.get("complete") or "") == "1"]


def batch_of(run):
    """First token of the run name.  Case SENSITIVE: `PP` and `pp` are two
    different submissions and must not be merged."""
    return re.split(r"[-_]", str(run))[0]


def in_family(r):
    """The matched family: everything but the box and alpha0/augment."""
    return (r.get("network") == NET and r.get("dataset") == DSET
            and r.get("base") == BASE_ALG and r.get("meta") == META_ALG
            and r.get("meta_stepsize") == MS
            and r.get("batch_size") == BATCH and r.get("gamma") == GAMMA
            and not (r.get("hier") or "").strip()
            and (r.get("collapsed") or "0") == "0"
            and str(r.get("epochs_done")) == str(EPOCHS)
            and r.get("granularity") in ARMS)


def canonical_batches(rows, alpha0, augment):
    """Rule 2(a)-(f).  Returns (kept, dropped) as {batch: {arm: [rows]}}."""
    pool = collections.defaultdict(lambda: collections.defaultdict(list))
    for r in rows:
        if not in_family(r):
            continue
        if r.get("alpha0") != alpha0 or r.get("augment") != augment:
            continue
        if r.get("beta_clip") != CANON_CLIP:
            continue
        pool[batch_of(r["run"])][r["granularity"]].append(r)
    kept, dropped = {}, {}
    for b, g in pool.items():
        if all(a in g and g[a] for a in ARMS):
            kept[b] = g
        else:
            dropped[b] = sorted(g)
    return kept, dropped


# =============================================================================
# NOISE FLOOR -- re-derived here, never quoted
# =============================================================================
def sigma_w_from_corpus(rows):
    """Pooled within-(batch x arm x alpha0 x augment x box) SD of CSV plateau5
    over the matched family, ANY box, ANY alpha0."""
    cells = collections.defaultdict(list)
    for r in rows:
        if not in_family(r):
            continue
        try:
            cells[(batch_of(r["run"]), r["granularity"], r["alpha0"],
                   r["augment"], r["beta_clip"])].append(float(r["plateau5"]))
        except (TypeError, ValueError):
            continue
    ss, df, nc = 0.0, 0, 0
    for v in cells.values():
        if len(v) >= 2:
            m = statistics.mean(v)
            ss += sum((x - m) ** 2 for x in v)
            df += len(v) - 1
            nc += 1
    return (math.sqrt(ss / df) if df else float("nan")), df, nc


def _gap_groups(rows, narrow):
    """Within-batch (arm - scalar) gaps at the CANONICAL box, grouped by the
    cell they belong to.  narrow=True restricts to the matched family."""
    pool = collections.defaultdict(lambda: collections.defaultdict(list))
    for r in rows:
        if r.get("beta_clip") != CANON_CLIP:
            continue
        if r.get("granularity") not in ARMS:
            continue
        if (r.get("collapsed") or "0") != "0":
            continue
        if narrow and not in_family(r):
            continue
        key = (r.get("network"), r.get("dataset"), r.get("base"), r.get("meta"),
               r.get("meta_stepsize"), r.get("alpha0"), r.get("augment"),
               r.get("epochs_done"), r.get("batch_size"), r.get("gamma"),
               (r.get("hier") or "").strip())
        try:
            pool[key][(batch_of(r["run"]), r["granularity"])].append(
                float(r["plateau5"]))
        except (TypeError, ValueError):
            continue
    groups = collections.defaultdict(list)
    for key, cells in pool.items():
        batches = sorted({b for (b, _a) in cells})
        for arm in PARTITIONED:
            gaps = []
            for b in batches:
                if (b, arm) in cells and (b, SCALAR) in cells:
                    gaps.append(statistics.mean(cells[(b, arm)])
                                - statistics.mean(cells[(b, SCALAR)]))
            if len(gaps) >= 2:
                groups[(key, arm)] = gaps
    return groups


def sigma_ba_from_corpus(rows, narrow):
    groups = _gap_groups(rows, narrow)
    ss, df, ng = 0.0, 0, 0
    for gaps in groups.values():
        m = statistics.mean(gaps)
        ss += sum((x - m) ** 2 for x in gaps)
        df += len(gaps) - 1
        ng += 1
    return (math.sqrt(ss / df) if df else float("nan")), df, ng

def _tokens(line):
    import shlex
    try:
        return shlex.split(line)
    except ValueError:
        return line.split()


def parse_args_line(line):
    toks = _tokens(line)
    eff, seen = {}, collections.Counter()
    i = 0
    while i < len(toks):
        t = toks[i]
        if t.startswith("--"):
            key, val = t[2:], "1"
            if i + 1 < len(toks) and not toks[i + 1].startswith("--"):
                val = toks[i + 1]
                i += 1
            seen[key] += 1
            eff[key] = val
        i += 1
    return eff, sorted(k for k, c in seen.items() if c > 1)


def parse_env_line(line):
    out = {}
    for tok in (line or "").split():
        if "=" in tok:
            k, v = tok.split("=", 1)
            out[k] = v
    return out


def read_out(path):
    args = env = None
    test, train = {}, {}
    try:
        with open(path, "r", errors="replace") as fh:
            for line in fh:
                if args is None:
                    m = ARGS_RE.match(line)
                    if m:
                        args = m.group(1).strip()
                        continue
                if env is None:
                    m = ENV_RE.match(line)
                    if m:
                        env = m.group(1).strip()
                        continue
                m = EP_RE.search(line)
                if m:
                    test[int(m.group(1))] = float(m.group(2))
                m = EPTR_RE.search(line)
                if m:
                    train[int(m.group(1))] = float(m.group(2))
    except IOError:
        return None
    if args is None:
        return None
    eff, dup = parse_args_line(args)
    return {"args": eff, "dup": dup, "argsline": args,
            "env": parse_env_line(env), "envline": env or "",
            "test": test, "train": train, "path": path}


def tail5(series, budget=EPOCHS, w=WINDOW):
    """PRIMARY window: mean of the LAST 5 epochs.  train.py prints 0-indexed."""
    v = [series[e] for e in range(budget - w, budget) if e in series]
    return sum(v) / len(v) if len(v) == w else None


def index_outs(dirs):
    """run-name -> .out path.  Disk names use '-' where the CSV uses '_'."""
    idx = {}
    for d in dirs:
        if not os.path.isdir(d):
            continue
        for fn in sorted(os.listdir(d)):
            if not fn.endswith(".out"):
                continue
            p = os.path.join(d, fn)
            rec = read_out(p)
            if rec is None:
                continue
            nm = rec["args"].get("run-name")
            if nm:
                idx[nm] = p
    return idx

def probe_binding(probe_dir, lo, hi):
    """FULL streaming pass over probe.jsonl -- every record, not a sample.
    Returns dict or None if unreadable."""
    p = os.path.join(probe_dir, "probe.jsonl")
    if not os.path.isfile(p):
        return None
    lo32, hi32 = f32(lo), f32(hi)
    n_rec = 0
    lo_hit = hi_hit = 0
    counted = 0          # records carrying n_at_lo / n_at_hi
    try:
        with open(p, "r", errors="replace") as fh:
            for line in fh:
                line = line.strip()
                if not line:
                    continue
                try:
                    rec = json.loads(line)
                except ValueError:
                    continue
                n_rec += 1
                used = False
                if "n_at_lo" in rec and "n_at_hi" in rec:
                    counted += 1
                    used = True
                    try:
                        if float(rec["n_at_lo"]) > 0:
                            lo_hit += 1
                        if float(rec["n_at_hi"]) > 0:
                            hi_hit += 1
                    except (TypeError, ValueError):
                        used = False
                if not used:
                    bmin = rec.get("beta_true_min")
                    bmax = rec.get("beta_true_max")
                    try:
                        if bmin is not None and abs(float(bmin) - lo32) <= WALL_TOL:
                            lo_hit += 1
                        if bmax is not None and abs(float(bmax) - hi32) <= WALL_TOL:
                            hi_hit += 1
                    except (TypeError, ValueError):
                        pass
    except IOError:
        return None
    if n_rec == 0:
        return None
    return {"n_records": n_rec, "n_with_counts": counted,
            "lo_records": lo_hit, "hi_records": hi_hit,
            "binds": (lo_hit > 0 or hi_hit > 0)}


def resolve_probe_dir(rec, probe_roots):
    """The run's OWN ENV PROBE_DIR is an exact path join (CORRECTIONS 148.3);
    fall back to matching its basename under a supplied --probe-root."""
    pd = rec["env"].get("PROBE_DIR")
    if pd and os.path.isdir(pd):
        return pd
    if pd:
        base = os.path.basename(pd.rstrip("/"))
        for root in probe_roots:
            cand = os.path.join(root, base)
            if os.path.isdir(cand):
                return cand
    return None


# =============================================================================
# SE arithmetic
# =============================================================================
def se_gap(sigma_w, n_arm, n_sc):
    return sigma_w * math.sqrt(1.0 / n_arm + 1.0 / n_sc)


def se_dgap(sigma_w, sigma_ba, n_a_rel, n_s_rel, can_ns):
    """can_ns = [(n_arm, n_scalar), ...] one entry per canonical batch."""
    B = len(can_ns)
    seed = (1.0 / n_a_rel + 1.0 / n_s_rel)
    seed += sum(1.0 / a + 1.0 / s for (a, s) in can_ns) / float(B * B)
    se_seed = sigma_w * math.sqrt(seed)
    se_batch = sigma_ba * math.sqrt(1.0 + 1.0 / B)
    return math.sqrt(se_seed ** 2 + se_batch ** 2), se_seed, se_batch

def selftest():
    ok = []

    def chk(name, cond):
        ok.append((name, bool(cond)))

    chk("f32(-2.3026) != -2.3026 (the CORRECTIONS 148.3 trap)",
        f32(-2.3026) != -2.3026)
    chk("f32(-15.0) == -15.0 (floor is exactly representable)",
        f32(-15.0) == -15.0)
    chk("f32 trap magnitude < 1e-6", abs(f32(-2.3026) - (-2.3026)) < 1e-6)
    chk("WALL_TOL swallows the f32 gap", abs(f32(-2.3026) - (-2.3026)) < WALL_TOL)

    # tail5 window
    s = {e: float(e) for e in range(EPOCHS)}
    chk("tail5 = mean of epochs 95..99", abs(tail5(s) - 97.0) < 1e-9)
    chk("tail5 refuses a short series", tail5({0: 1.0}) is None)

    # ARGS parsing keeps the LAST occurrence and reports the repeat
    eff, dup = parse_args_line("--alpha0 1e-3 --alpha0 1e-4 --seed 0")
    chk("repeated flag detected", dup == ["alpha0"])
    chk("argparse semantics: last wins", eff["alpha0"] == "1e-4")

    # batch_of is case sensitive: PP and pp are two submissions
    chk("batch_of case sensitive", batch_of("PP_scal_s0") != batch_of("pp_scal_s0"))
    chk("batch_of on hyphen names", batch_of("tc1-ly-a3-s0") == "tc1")
    chk("batch_of on underscore names", batch_of("a0-scal-1e3_s0") == "a0")

    # NEITHER released cell is a one-factor manipulation -- section 6 (ii)
    clo, chi = parse_clip(CANON_CLIP)
    one_factor = []
    for k, c in RELEASED.items():
        lo, hi = parse_clip(c["clip"])
        moved = (abs(lo - clo) > 1e-9) + (abs(hi - chi) > 1e-9)
        one_factor.append(moved == 1)
        chk("%s moves BOTH walls (floor %+.4f, ceiling %+.4f nats)"
            % (k, lo - clo, hi - chi), moved == 2)
    chk("NO released cell is one-factor -> SETTLED is unreachable",
        not any(one_factor))

    # SE arithmetic
    s_gap = se_gap(1.0, 2, 2)
    chk("se_gap(sigma=1,n=2,2) = 1.0", abs(s_gap - 1.0) < 1e-12)
    tot, sd_, sb_ = se_dgap(1.0, 0.0, 2, 2, [(3, 3)])
    chk("se_dgap with sigma_BA=0 reduces to seed term",
        abs(tot - math.sqrt(1.0 + 2.0 / 3.0)) < 1e-12 and abs(sb_) < 1e-12)
    tot2, _, sb2 = se_dgap(0.0, 1.0, 2, 2, [(3, 3), (3, 3)])
    chk("batch floor does NOT shrink with seeds",
        abs(tot2 - math.sqrt(1.5)) < 1e-12 and sb2 > 0)
    tot3, _, _ = se_dgap(1.0, 1.0, 3, 3, [(3, 3), (3, 3)])
    chk("total SE >= batch floor always", tot3 >= math.sqrt(1.5) - 1e-12)

    # bars are in SE units
    chk("RESOLVE_K is 2 SE", RESOLVE_K == 2.0)
    chk("MDE_K is 2.8 SE", MDE_K == 2.8)
    chk("NOISY_K is 3 sigma_w", NOISY_K == 3.0)
    chk("DEAD_BAR above CIFAR-10 chance (10.00)", DEAD_BAR > 10.0)
    chk("occupancy VOID bar above FLAG bar", OCC_VOID_SPREAD > OCC_FLAG_SPREAD)

    # branch coverage: all three registered
    chk("three roll-up branches registered", BRANCH_A_MIN == 4 and BRANCH_B_MIN == 4
        and BRANCH_B_MIN_COLLAPSED == 2)

    # the scorer must not be able to emit a settlement
    chk("no released cell reaches n>=3 AND one-factor",
        not any((c["n_per"] >= 3 and
                 sum([abs(parse_clip(c["clip"])[0] - clo) > 1e-9,
                      abs(parse_clip(c["clip"])[1] - chi) > 1e-9]) == 1)
                for c in RELEASED.values()))

    npass = sum(1 for _n, c in ok if c)
    for n, c in ok:
        print("  %-4s %s" % ("PASS" if c else "FAIL", n))
    print("selftest: %d/%d PASS" % (npass, len(ok)))
    return 0 if npass == len(ok) else 2

                  % (gap_rel, m_a_rel[4], m_s_rel[4]))
            print("       GAP canonical per batch = %s"
                  % ", ".join("%s %+.4f" % (b, g)
                              for b, g in zip(sorted(can_by), gaps_can)))
            print("       GAP canonical (mean of within-batch gaps) = %+8.4f pp"
                  % gap_can)
            print("       D_GAP = released - canonical              = %+8.4f pp"
                  % dgap)
            print("       SE_GAP(released) %s pp -> RESOLVE_GAP = %.1f SE = %s pp"
                  % (fmt(sg_rel), RESOLVE_K, fmt(rb_gap)))
            print("       SE_D = sqrt(seed %s^2 + batch %s^2) = %s pp"
                  % (fmt(sd_seed), fmt(sd_batch), fmt(sd_tot)))
            print("       RESOLVE_D = %.1f SE = %s pp    MDE(80%%) = %.1f SE = %s pp"
                  % (RESOLVE_K, fmt(rb_d), MDE_K, fmt(mde)))
            print("       |D_GAP| / SE_D = %s SE" % fmt(abs(dgap) / sd_tot if sd_tot else float("nan"), 2))

            gap_resolvable = abs(gap_rel) >= rb_gap
            if dgap > rb_d:
                out = "INFLATED"
            elif dgap < -rb_d:
                out = "REDUCED" if (gap_rel > 0 and gap_resolvable) else "COLLAPSED"
            else:
                out = "SURVIVES" if (gap_rel > 0 and gap_resolvable) else "UNRESOLVED"
            mods = []
            if abs(dgap) < rb_d:
                mods.append("NULL-AMBIGUOUS-TWO-WALL")
            if occ_flag:
                mods.append("OCCUPANCY-FLAGGED spread %.3f" % spread)
            mods.append("CROSS-BATCH")
            if min(m_a_rel[4], m_s_rel[4]) < 3:
                mods.append("UNDERPOWERED n=%d DIRECTION-CHECK-ONLY"
                            % min(m_a_rel[4], m_s_rel[4]))
            if spec["prefix"] == "ub9":
                mods.append("CROSS-ACCOUNT (released cluster_b / canonical cluster_a)")
            print("       OUTCOME: %s   [%s]" % (out, "; ".join(mods)))
            results.append({"cell": cell_key, "arm": arm, "outcome": out,
                            "gap_rel": gap_rel, "gap_can": gap_can, "dgap": dgap,
                            "se_d": sd_tot, "mods": mods})

    # ---------------- roll-up ---------------------------------------------
    print("\n" + "=" * 78)
    print("ROLL-UP FOR MASTER-TABLE ROW 24")
    print("=" * 78)
    if void_all:
        for k, why in void_all:
            print("  cell %-12s -> %s" % (k, why))
    n_tot = len(results)
    cnt = collections.Counter(r["outcome"] for r in results)
    print("\n  scored contrasts: %d of 6 registered" % n_tot)
    for k in ("SURVIVES", "INFLATED", "REDUCED", "COLLAPSED", "UNRESOLVED"):
        print("    %-11s %d" % (k, cnt.get(k, 0)))

    surv = cnt.get("SURVIVES", 0) + cnt.get("INFLATED", 0)
    coll = cnt.get("COLLAPSED", 0)
    redu = cnt.get("REDUCED", 0)
    if n_tot == 6 and surv >= BRANCH_A_MIN and coll == 0:
        branch = "BRANCH A -- ROW 24 STANDS ON THESE CELLS"
    elif n_tot == 6 and (coll + redu) >= BRANCH_B_MIN and coll >= BRANCH_B_MIN_COLLAPSED:
        branch = "BRANCH B -- ROW 24 MUST BE REWRITTEN"
    else:
        branch = "BRANCH C -- OPEN"
    print("\n  %s" % branch)

    print("\n  MODIFIERS ON THE VERDICT (registered as branches, not footnotes):")
    amb = [r for r in results if "NULL-AMBIGUOUS-TWO-WALL" in r["mods"]]
    print("    NULL-AMBIGUOUS-TWO-WALL on %d/%d contrasts.  NEITHER released cell"
          % (len(amb), n_tot))
    print("      is one-factor: tc1 -30:6.0 and ub9 -60:6.0 each move BOTH walls")
    print("      against the canonical -15:-2.3026, so a null cannot separate")
    print("      'the box does not matter' from 'the two changes cancel'.")
    print("    CROSS-BATCH-WEAKNESS on ALL %d contrasts.  %s" % (n_tot, XB))
    print("    tc1 is n=2 per (arm, alpha0) -> DIRECTION CHECK, never a settlement.")

    print("\n  THIS SCORER CANNOT PRINT A SETTLEMENT VERDICT.  A settlement would")
    print("  require every cell at n>=3 AND a ONE-FACTOR box manipulation; the")
    print("  second condition is false for both released cells by arithmetic on")
    print("  their own ENV lines, so no contrast here is eligible.  Every verdict")
    print("  token is a DIRECTION reading.")
    print("\nVERDICT: DIRECTION-%s" % branch.split("--")[0].strip().replace(" ", "-"))
    return 0
