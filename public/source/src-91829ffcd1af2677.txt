## 2. THE CELL — `gn1`'s cell, and the two box corrections

### 2.1 The configuration, held byte-identical across every arm (RULE 10)

```
--optimizer HF  --alg-base SGDm  --momentum-param-base 0.99  --weight-decay-base 0.1
--alg-meta Lion --momentum-param-meta 0.99 --Lion-beta2-meta 0.9 --weight-decay-meta 0
--dataset CIFAR10  --batch-size 100  --gamma 1  --alpha0 1e-3
--meta-stepsize 1e-4        <-- see 2.2/2.3
--num-epochs 100            <-- see 2.4
AUGMENT=1   BETA_CLIP=-15:-2.3026   HIER unset   PROBE=5  PROBE5=1   <-- see 2.2/2.6
```

Only two fields vary: `--NN-name` and `--stepsize-groups`.

### 2.2 Why `ms = 1e-4`, and why the floor choice is free

`HF.py:676` `Lion_meta_update` with `weight_decay_meta = 0` gives |Δβ| = `ms` **exactly**,
per coordinate, per step. CIFAR-10 at batch 100 is 500 steps/epoch, so over 100 epochs the
total reachable excursion from β₀ = log(1e-3) = −6.907755 is `500 × 100 × ms`:

| ms | max ‖Δβ‖ | reachable β interval | floor −15 | floor −25 | ceiling −2.3026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1e-4 | 5.0 | [−11.9078, −1.9078] | needs 80,922 of 50,000 steps → **unreachable** | unreachable | reachable |
| 3e-4 | 15.0 | [−21.9078, +8.0922] | reached at step 26,974 of 50,000 | unreachable | reachable |

So **at ms = 1e-4 the floor is provably dead and `-25` and `-15` are identical in effect**,
and the box question reduces entirely to the ceiling, which is reachable (from epoch 92.1) and
is **not** released: the corpus's only released-ceiling runs (`bd7-w-c6-s0/s1`, box `-30:6.0`)
are 2/2 `collapsed=1`. **Which of the two identical floors to write down is settled in §2.6.**

### 2.3 THE CORRECTION — `ms = 3e-4` HAS NO CLEAN BOX, AND THE FEASIBILITY PASS HAD THIS WRONG

The feasibility pass recommended running the ladder at `ms = 3e-4`, `BETA_CLIP -25:-2.3026`,
on the stated ground that box occupancy there reads `rec_lo = rec_hi = 0.0000 on 12/12`.
**I re-measured every relevant `probe.jsonl` (10,000 records each) and that is false.** What
is actually on disk (`rec` = fraction of probe records with ≥1 β at the guard):

| batch | ms | box | arm | `rec_lo` | `rec_hi` | worst coord count |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ar1` | 3e-4 | −15:−2.3026 | chunk777 / chunk2325 / nodewise1d | **0.4560–0.4582** | 0.0000 | 19–27 of 14,4xx |
| `ar1` | 3e-4 | −15:−2.3026 | **nodewise** | **0.4521–0.4596** | 0.0000–0.1195 | **1,699–1,748** of 14,420 |
| `fa1` | 3e-4 | −25:−2.3026 | chunk777 / chunk2325 / nodewise1d | 0.0000 (18/18) | 0.0000 (18/18) | 0 |
| `fa1` | 3e-4 | −25:−2.3026 | **nodewise** | 0.0000 | **0.0000 / 0.1672 / 0.0023 / 0.0137 / 0.2192 / 0.0600** (s0…s5) | 1–4 of 14,420 |
| `mm1`,`pp1`,`bn1`,`cc1` | **1e-4** | −15:−2.3026 | **all four granularities, 33 probe arms** | **0.0000 (33/33)** | **0.0000 (33/33)** | 0; max β reached = **−3.32**, a full 1.0 nat below the ceiling |

Reading, stated plainly: at `ms = 3e-4` **there is no box that is free for the singleton
arm.** Closing the floor at −15 pins ~12 % of `nodewise`'s coordinates there; releasing it to
−25 frees the floor but the meta-learner then runs the other way and `nodewise` — and *only*
`nodewise*, the arm that loses the contrast — touches the **ceiling** on 3 of 6 seeds above
R4's 5 %-of-records threshold. The bind is small in coordinates (1–4 β of 14,420, i.e.
0.007–0.028 %, versus `ar1`'s 1,700), so `fa1`'s D is not thereby void; but it is nonzero,
it is **arm-asymmetric in the same direction as the effect**, and under RULE 5 that makes it
a caveat that must travel with `fa1`'s +0.629 wherever it is quoted.

**Consequence, registered:** both batches run at **`ms = 1e-4`**, the only cell where both
guards read exactly zero on every arm and every seed of every batch that owns probes, with a
measured 1.0-nat margin to the reachable ceiling. This overrides the feasibility pass's cell
for the ladder. The price is paid in §4.6: the anchor is the ms=1e-4 pooled D, not `fa1`'s.

### 2.4 Why 100 epochs

Fixed, not chosen per arm. (a) Every anchor this registration predicts against — `mm1`,
`pp1`, `bn1`, `cc1`, `fa1` — is a 100-epoch batch, and `plateau5` is the mean of the last 5
test epochs, so it is a **budget-relative** statistic and is not comparable across budgets
(this is also why the CSV's `plateau` column, mean-of-last-20, is never used here). (b) The
reachability arithmetic in 2.2 is a function of `500 × epochs × ms`; changing the budget
changes the box occupancy and would void the clean cell established in 2.3. (c) 100 epochs
is the corpus's modal budget for ResNet18 (median wall-clock **42 min**, n = 1,068, range
28–115), so cost is known rather than estimated.

### 2.5 The primary metric

**`plateau5`** — mean of the last 5 test epochs read from the `.out` series. Contrasts are
**Welch, unpaired**. Seeds do **not** pair across batches on this cluster, so every registered
contrast below is formed **within one batch**; no contrast in this document crosses a batch
boundary except where explicitly labelled as an anchor comparison, and those carry their own
inflated uncertainty.

### 2.6 THE SECOND BOX CORRECTION — **−25 → −15**, made at cycle-84 review

§2.2 proves the two floors are identical *in effect*. It does not follow that the choice is
free of consequence for the **thresholds**, and that is what the first draft missed.

**`(ms = 1e-4, −25:-2.3026)` exists in ZERO of the 1,891 corpus rows.** Re-derived at write
time: every ms = 1e-4 run sits at `-15:-2.3026` (`nodewise` 16, `chunk777` 9, `nodewise1d` 6,
`chunk2325` 6), and the only `-25` rows in the corpus are `fa1`'s, at ms = 3e-4. Meanwhile
**every frozen number in `gn1`** — the +0.30 T1 gate, the +0.5805 anchor, σ = 0.1740, and
therefore the powered-null threshold — is computed from −15 data. Running the batch at −25
would have made each one a **cross-box import**, which is exactly the move CORRECTIONS 113
forbids after `fa1` showed the box changes the *optimiser* and not merely the instrument
(`nodewise1d` moved −0.177, t −2.22, RESOLVED).

Moving to −15 costs nothing by §2.2's proof and buys a cell that is **byte-identical to the
anchors**. It also gains direct empirical support on the arm that matters most: at this exact
cell the `nodewise` (singleton) arm — the one that binds hardest everywhere else — reads
`rec_lo = rec_hi = 0.0000` on **12/12** probe arms (mm1 s0–2, pp1 s0–2, cc1 s3–5, tw0 s0–2),
re-measured from `probes_*/probe.jsonl` at write time. Guard 3 now asserts the box against the
**anchor** box rather than against `fa1`'s, and it was tested in both directions, including the
case where the script *and* the registry are both moved to −25 together (§4.9).

## 3. IDEA A → **`sl1` IS WITHDRAWN.** The fixed-m ladder was built, validated, and killed

`bin/c84_singleton_ladder.sh` and `analysis/c84_sl1_score.py` are **DELETED**. `sl1` is
**deliberately left unregistered** in `analysis/c55_neff_noise.BOXES`, with the reason
recorded there. `patches/patch_chunkvec.py` and `tests/test_chunkvec.py` are **RETAINED** —
see §3.4.

This is not a retreat from a hard result. The batch was finished: a per-tensor chunk-vector
patch that subsumed four existing code paths and reproduced each of them **bitwise** (68
checks, 0 failures, on the real ResNet18), a 105/105 scorer selftest, a beta-allocation guard
that measured `m` on every rung from the *allocated* β, and RULE-13 both-directions evidence
on 16 guard checks. It was killed anyway, on arithmetic that was re-derived rather than
argued.

### 3.1 DEFECT 1 — holding *m* fixed does not remove the collinearity, it **relocates** it

The ladder's own identity is `m = G2(K2) + S_k + (41 − k)`, where `G2` is the number of chunk
groups on the 21 tensors with ndim ≥ 2 and `S_k` the number of injected size-1 groups. Fixing
`m` therefore **forces** `dG2 = −dS + dk`, and `|dk| ≤ 41` against `dS` spanning **9,610**.
Recomputed over the seven registered rungs:

| rung | k | K2 | **G2** | **S** | m | f |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L0 | 0 | 777 | 14,380 | 0 | 14,421 | 0.00000 |
| L1 | 7 | 802 | 13,927 | 448 | 14,409 | 0.03109 |
| L2 | 14 | 844 | 13,237 | 1,152 | 14,416 | 0.07991 |
| L3 | 21 | 914 | 12,226 | 2,176 | 14,422 | 0.15088 |
| L4 | 28 | 1070 | 10,442 | 3,968 | 14,423 | 0.27512 |
| L5 | 34 | 1417 | 7,893 | 6,528 | 14,428 | 0.45245 |
| L6 | 41 | 2325 | 4,810 | 9,610 | 14,420 | 0.66644 |

**corr(S, G2) = −0.9999975.** That is the *same order* as the `corr(S, m) = 0.9999991` that
this document already used (§5.1) to kill the naive ladder as dead on arrival. The ladder has
**one** degree of freedom, and "singletons hurt" and "coarse ndim ≥ 2 chunks hurt" are two
names for it. **No rung in the design distinguishes them, and none could:** it is a theorem
about any fixed-*m* ladder, not an artefact of the chosen K2 values. The header's claim that
"there is no comparator here to move" was wrong in a specific way — the comparator did not
disappear, it was **absorbed into the treatment arm**.

The defence §3.4 offered was that the ndim ≥ 2 axis is independently pinned at ~0. That
defence is legitimate in *kind* but not in *strength*. At the registered cell the only reading
is `cc1`'s `chunk777 − chunk2325` = **−0.101, se 0.190, t −0.53** (n = 3v3) — **unresolved**,
with a ~95 % envelope of roughly [−0.71, +0.50]. The other two draws (+0.031 `ar1`, −0.019
`fa1`) are both at ms = 3e-4, the cell this very document forbids pooling with. Quoting the
point estimate of an unresolved statistic as a bound, and then hard-coding "it cannot
MANUFACTURE one" into the scorer's selftest, is having it both ways.

**Consequence for the verdict, and this is the part that matters:** the collinearity does not
break the CONFIRMED branch (the nuisance is signed *against* the prediction at `cc1`, so a
resolved negative slope stays conservative). It breaks the **REFUTED** branch, which the
registration left uncovered. A true singleton slope of −0.3 pp per unit f cancelled by an
ndim ≥ 2 slope of +0.3 would produce `b̂ ≈ 0` with se ≤ 0.16, and the scorer would print
**REFUTED — "the tail mechanism is wrong at fixed m"** for a cancellation it cannot see.

### 3.2 DEFECT 2 — the registered primary could not fail on the question it was built to ask

The primary was `b̂`, the OLS slope of plateau5 on f across the seven rungs, registered
negative. On the registered f grid (f̄ = 0.23656, Sxx = 0.36295, se(b̂) = 0.1179 at n = 6),
with the two endpoints pinned at their predicted values (L0 = 0, L6 = −0.581), the CONFIRMED
gate (`b̂ ≤ −0.45`, `t ≤ −2`, span in [+0.30, +0.90]) was driven directly and returns:

| interior shape | b̂ | t | CONFIRMED? |
|---|---|---|---|
| true linear dose-response | −0.872 | −7.4 | **yes** |
| **pure STEP at L6** (no dose-response at all) | −0.688 | −5.8 | **yes** |
| **NON-MONOTONE zigzag** | −0.565 | −4.8 | **yes** |
| threshold at L4 | −1.095 | −9.3 | yes |
| **saturating** — a *genuine* dose-response, all of it by L1 | −0.379 | −3.2 | **no** |

**The gate is miscalibrated in both directions.** It fires for two shapes that are not
dose-responses and fails the one shape that is most mechanistically interesting. The reason is
structural: OLS leverage on this grid is `|w|` = 17.3 / 15.0 / 11.4 / 6.3 / 2.8 / 15.8 /
31.4 %, so **L0 and L6 together carry 48.7 %** — and *both endpoints are already in the CSV*.
L0 **is** `chunk777` by exact identity (largest one-D numel 512 < 777); L6 is `nodewise` up to
the ndim ≥ 2 alignment term, which is what `G` measures. The span the ladder was registered to
reproduce is `D − G`, both of which the campaign already owns. Meanwhile T4.2 and T4.3
**forbade** reporting rung ordering, monotonicity, adjacent pairs, or any functional form —
because at n = 6 none of them is resolvable. **Thirty of the forty-eight jobs bought a
quantity the batch had pre-committed to never state, and the remaining eighteen re-measured
`D`.**

### 3.3 A third reason, sufficient on its own: the refutation was worth little

A flat ladder would have removed a *mechanistic gloss*, not a claim. The paper's contribution
is the **endpoint prescription** — "do not give BatchNorm scalars their own step sizes" —
which is established at matched count five times over (`D` IV-pooled **+0.627, se 0.063**, 18
within-batch seed pairs, five batches, two meta-stepsizes, two boxes) and is untouched by a
flat interior. No reviewer asks whether the penalty is linear in the number of singletons.

### 3.4 THE REGISTERED SUCCESSOR — a 2×2 factorial with *m* floating, **not built, not costed**

Recorded so the kill does not have to be re-derived, and so the retained patch has a stated
purpose. **`m` cannot be held fixed AND the collinearity broken — that is the identity.** So
let `m` float and run a factorial in (S, G2) instead of a 1-D diagonal:

| cell | granularity | S | G2 | m |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | `chunkvec777s0` (= `chunk777`) | 0 | 14,380 | 14,421 |
| B | `chunkvec2325s0` (= `chunk2325`) | 0 | 4,810 | 4,851 |
| C | `chunkvec777s41` | 9,610 | 14,380 | 23,990 |
| D | `chunkvec2325s41` (= ladder rung L6) | 9,610 | 4,810 | 14,420 |

The **S main effect at fixed G2** is `½[(C−A) + (D−B)]` — a real contrast that can genuinely
return null, unlike a diagonal slope. `A − B` measures the ndim ≥ 2 / *m* axis **in-batch, at
the registered cell, at the batch's own n**, instead of importing `cc1`'s se-0.190 draw; that
single arm is what converts the design from re-confounded to de-confounded. `m` becomes a
measured covariate rather than a constraint. **This is a sketch, not a registration.** Before
it may be built it needs its own power computation, its own frozen bands, its own treatment of
the *m* nuisance (ck1 measures −0.407 pp per decade of m, and `C − A` spans 0.221 decades
while `D − B` spans 0.473 — those are **not** equal, so the two half-contrasts carry different
nuisances and the design must say how it handles that), and its own registration document.
It needs `PATCH_CHUNKVEC`, which is why that patch and its suite are retained — but the patch
**must not be applied to the live tree until the successor exists**, and its operator note now
says so.

## 4. IDEA B → BATCH **`gn1`** — NORMALISER TRANSFER AT FIXED SINGLETON STRUCTURE

### 4.1 The question, and what it is *not*

*Is the partition gap carried by the degenerate size-1 tail **per se**, or by **BatchNorm**
specifically?*

It is **not** a test of "does a network without normalisation show a partition gap". It
removes **zero** singletons. §4.7 T3.2 forbids writing it up as one.

### 4.2 Why the test is unusually tight — verified, not assumed

`nn.GroupNorm(G, C)` has `weight` and `bias` of shape `(C,)`, exactly as `nn.BatchNorm2d(C)`.
BN's `running_mean`/`running_var` are **buffers**: they never appear in `named_parameters()`,
so they never received a step size under any granularity and their absence changes nothing.
I re-derived the census for a GroupNorm ResNet18 and it is **identical to the BN row in every
column**: 11,173,962 params · nodewise m 14,420 · 9,610 singletons · f 66.6436 % · 0.086004 %
weight coverage · nodewise1d m 4,851 · 41 one-D tensors · same count-matched K = 777 → 14,421.

`grep` over `train.py` and `Optimizers/HF.py` returns **no module-type logic whatsoever** — no
`BatchNorm`, no `named_modules`, no `isinstance`-on-modules. `HF.init_meta` is driven entirely
by `[(name, p.data.size()) for name,p in net.named_parameters()]`. The partition is therefore
not merely similar across the two nets; it is **provably the same object**, and **no optimiser
patch is required**. Exactly one thing in the experiment changes.

`G = 32` is buildable everywhere: all ResNet18 channel counts are 64/128/256/512, each
divisible by 32, including the three downsample shortcuts. **LayerNorm is rejected** — on conv
activations it is `GroupNorm(1, C)`, the same caveat below in its most extreme form with no
compensating advantage.

### 4.3 The arms and their computed group counts

All four **in one batch**, so `D_BN` and `D_GN` are within-batch and the cross-batch offset
(three draws, no stable sign) cancels.

| arm | `--NN-name` | `--stepsize-groups` | **m** |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bn-node` | `ResNet18` | `nodewise` | **14,420** |
| `bn-ch` | `ResNet18` | `chunk777` | **14,421** |
| `gn-node` | `ResNet18_gn` | `nodewise` | **14,420** |
| `gn-ch` | `ResNet18_gn` | `chunk777` | **14,421** |

Count mismatch **+1 group in 14,420 = 0.007 %**, identical to every previous reading of `D`.

### 4.4 The comparator

`bn-ch` − `bn-node` is the comparator for `gn-ch` − `gn-node`, in the same batch. It is a
**gate**, not a result (§4.7 T1): a batch whose own BatchNorm control fails to show the effect
cannot be asked whether the effect transfers.

### 4.5 The registered statistics

* **PRIMARY** `D_GN = plateau5(gn-ch) − plateau5(gn-node)`, within-batch, Welch, **8v8**.
* **CO-PRIMARY / GATE** `D_BN = plateau5(bn-ch) − plateau5(bn-node)`, same batch, **4v4**.
  It is a *gate*, so it carries the smaller n (§4.6).
* **SECONDARY** `ΔD = D_GN − D_BN`.
* **T0.6 COMMENSURABILITY GATE — ADDED AT CYCLE-84 REVIEW, evaluated after T0.1–T0.5 and
  BEFORE T2.** `|mean plateau5 over the GN arms − mean over the BN arms| ≤ 2.0 pp`. The
  T0.3 floor of 85 does **not** protect this batch's arithmetic on its own: the whole test
  compares two differences **in percentage points** on two networks that need not sit at
  the same accuracy. BN plateaus near 92.2 (error budget 7.8 pp); a GN arm at 88.0 would
  pass T0.1–T0.5 cleanly while sitting on a **12.0 pp** budget — a 1.6× change in the scale
  the effect lives on. If the gap is even partly *multiplicative* in the budget, `D_GN` is
  distorted by ~60 %, about **7× se(`D_GN`)**, straddling **both** the +0.30 TRANSFERS line
  and the [−0.15, +0.15] null band, so a T2 firing could be a pure accuracy-regime artefact.
  **If it fires, the batch reports "GroupNorm sits in a different accuracy regime; `D_GN`
  and `D_BN` are not commensurable in pp" and issues NO transfer verdict. That is not a
  null.**
* **T2c ERROR-BUDGET-NORMALISED, DESCRIPTIVE ONLY, REPORTED ALWAYS.** `D / (100 − level)`
  per net, printed beside the pp numbers so a reader can see for themselves whether a
  verdict is a scale artefact. It **gates nothing**: registering two primaries would move
  the argmax problem up a level rather than solve it.
* **MANDATORY** all four arm means reported separately with sd, n and both occupancy channels.
  The absolute GN level is itself the headroom check.
* **NOT AVAILABLE AT THIS CELL — recorded so it is not reached for later.** The clip-severity
  corroboration (`coord_lo`, the time-averaged fraction of β at the floor) discriminates
  sharply on BN: re-derived from `probes_ar1`, `coord_lo(nodewise)` = **0.03430 / 0.03425 /
  0.03406** against **0.00039–0.00040** (chunk777), **0.00092–0.00093** (chunk2325) and
  **0.00138–0.00141** (nodewise1d) — a **24–88×** separation. But that is `ar1`'s cell
  (ms = 3e-4, floor −15). **At the registered cell no coordinate reaches either guard on any
  arm** (§2.3), so `coord_lo` is identically 0 and this channel yields nothing here. It may
  **not** be recovered by moving the batch to a binding box: that would trade an
  interpretable accuracy contrast for an uninterpretable one. Report `rec_lo`/`rec_hi` as the
  T0.5 gate only.

### 4.6 Seeds, power, epochs — **REBALANCED 6/6/6/6 → 4/4/8/8 AT CYCLE-84 REVIEW**

σ = **0.1740**, re-derived at write time as the pooled within-cell sd of plateau5 over 22 dof
and 11 arm-cells at this cell (guard 2d recomputes it at submit time and refuses to run if it
has moved by more than 0.03).

Half the original batch was a **sixth reading of `D`**. The BN control only has to **clear a
gate** (`t ≥ 2` against +0.5805); the GN arms carry the **primary**. So:

| arm pair | n | se | note |

invariance", and the registered disambiguator `GroupNorm(num_groups = C)` is a **smaller**
perturbation from GN(32,C) than the old text implied — which makes it a cheaper follow-up

byte-identical singleton structure, and all three readings below can only *suppress* a gap,

none of them:**

## 5. THE KILLED IDEAS — reasons on the record, so they are not re-proposed

### 5.1 KILLED: Idea A as sketched — the naive within-architecture singleton-count ladder

*Design:* k of the 41 one-D tensors keep per-channel treatment, 41−k collapse to one group
each, **ndim≥2 side left at nodewise**, each rung against a count-matched chunk comparator.

**REASON — perfect collinearity with m, arithmetically, not merely empirically.**
`m(k) = 4,810 + (41 − k) + S_k`, so `m − S_k = 4,851 − k`, confined to **[4,810, 4,851]**
across the entire ladder — 0.28 % of m at the top rung, 0.85 % at the bottom.
**`corr(S, m) = 0.9999991`** over all 42 rungs (re-derived). m runs 4,851 → 14,420, a factor
of **2.973**, and each step is exactly the un-collapsed tensor's channel count (+63 through the
ten width-64 tensors, +127, +255, +511, then +9 for `linear.bias`). The ladder's m-motion is
**100 % determined by its singleton-motion**; it measures count, not singletons.

No choice of *which* one-D tensors breaks it: the 41 tensors carry 9,610 scalars while k
contributes at most 41, so even the most extreme re-selection (ten width-512 tensors, S = 5,120
at k = 10, versus thirty-one small ones, S = 4,490 at k = 31) moves S and m apart by < 0.5 %.

The count-matched-comparator patch does **not** rescue it: the comparator's own ndim≥2 group
count must then sweep **4,810 → 14,380** as K goes 2325 → 777, so the measured gap mixes
singleton injection with a 3× change in the comparator's partition of **99.91 %** of the
weights, perfectly anti-correlated with the treatment. This is precisely defect 3 that
withdrew `sf1`. And the two endpoints of this ladder are the two contrasts already in hand
(`D` at k = 41, `G` at k = 0), so a naive interior ladder would add cost without adding one
de-confounded degree of freedom. **Its fixed-m replacement `sl1` was built and then KILLED TOO (§3): holding m fixed does not remove this collinearity, it relocates it to `G2` (corr(S, G2) = −0.9999975). Nothing on this axis is buildable without the §3.4 factorial.**

### 5.2 KILLED: Idea B as sketched — the BN-free ResNet falsification

*Design:* a ResNet18 with `nn.Identity()` in place of every `BatchNorm2d`; if the gap is
carried by singletons on normalisation parameters, it should vanish.

Technically cheap — ~12 additive lines threading a `norm` kwarg through `BasicBlock` and
`ResNet`, plus a `'ResNet18_nn'` dispatch key, and **zero** optimiser change (§4.2). Killed
anyway, for three reasons in order of force.

1. **IT RE-MEASURES A CONTRAST THE CAMPAIGN ALREADY OWNS.** A BN-free ResNet18 with
   `conv bias=False` has **11,164,362 params, nodewise m = 4,820, and TEN singletons**
   (`linear.bias` only), f = 0.208 %. That is not "a network with the tail removed by a
   partition choice" — it is a network whose nodewise partition **is already structurally
   tail-free** at m ≈ 4,820. The contrast it would measure (nodewise vs count-matched
   `chunk2320` at m ≈ 4,820) is, to within 31 groups of 4,850, **the same contrast `G`
   already measures at m = 4,851 EXACT**. `G` re-derived at write time: `bn1` **+0.295**
   (t 6.15) / `ar1` **−0.139** (t −1.94) / `fa1` **−0.001** (t −0.01) / `cc1` **+0.011**
   (t 0.08). It collapses. The BN-free arm's predicted answer is already in the CSV, obtained
   without an architecture change.
2. **THE ONLY INFORMATIVE OUTCOME IS UNINTERPRETABLE.** If the gap *persisted* at m ≈ 4,820
   you could not attribute it: you removed the singletons, the normalisation, the batch
   statistics, the scale invariance, ~9,600 parameters and the accuracy regime, all at once.
   No arm in the design separates them.
3. **THE TRAINING RISK IS REAL, UNMEASURED, AND ASYMMETRIC — stated, not papered over.**
   The corpus has **zero** evidence in either direction: all 1,891 runs are BatchNorm nets
   (ResNet18 1,470 · ResNet18_c100 168 · ResNet10 110 · ResNet34 105 · ResNet50 19 ·
   ResNet10_c100 9 · ResNet34_c100 9 · ResNet101 1); zero `M1`/`M2` runs; zero norm-free runs.
   What *is* decisive is arithmetic: |Δβ| = ms exactly per step, so α can move by at most
   1.051× per epoch at ms = 1e-4 and cutting α by 10× takes **46 epochs**. **There is no fast
   brake.** The meta-learner follows `sign(HᵀT ∇f)` — a greedy loss-descent signal with no
   stability term — toward a ceiling (α ≤ 0.1, two decades above α₀, reachable in ~46 epochs)
   that was calibrated on BN nets. A BN-free post-activation ResNet18 has residual-branch
   variance compounding across 8 blocks with no renormalisation and a far lower stable-LR
   ceiling. If it goes, it **cannot recover inside the budget**. The phenotype is already in
   the corpus: 14 of 1,891 runs are `collapsed=1`, and the released-ceiling pair
   `bd7-w-c6-s0/s1` trained to best_test 88.82/88.94 and ended at **final_test 10.0**.
   **Honest bottom line: I cannot say whether a BN-free ResNet18 reaches a usable plateau at
   this cell, and nobody can from this corpus.** RULE 11 would then require its own α₀/ms
   sweep, turning a 12-job test into a multi-batch programme. *"Cannot be tested without a
   training-stability confound, and the confound costs more to remove than the test is
   worth"* is the verdict.

**Two nearby variants, also considered, also not run.**
* **BN with `affine=False`** — trains fine, but gives the *identical* shape list to the
  BN-free net (11,164,362 params, m 4,820, 10 singletons), so it inherits defect 1 exactly:
  it re-measures `G` by deleting the tail parameters instead of merging them.
* **Norm-free with `conv bias=True`** — genuinely new: m **9,620**, **4,810 singletons**,
  f exactly **50.000 %**, count-matched K = 1164 → 9,621. The only design that separates
  "singletons" from "normalisation exists at all". But it carries the full training risk;
  there is **no anchor `D` at m = 9,620** (every owned `D` is at m ≈ 14,420 and every `G` at
  m ≈ 4,851, and the uniform-chunk ladder `ck1` measures **−0.407 pp per decade of m**
  (chunk128 m = 87,303 → 92.159; chunk1024 m = 10,944 → 92.526; re-derived), so accuracy at
  m ≈ 9,620 is not the same baseline and the magnitude is not transportable); and the cross-family f extrapolations that would supply a
  prediction are withdrawn. Sign prediction only, at high void risk. **Hold as the follow-up
  if and only if `gn1` returns the BN-specific null.**
* **`M1`/`M2`** (MNIST MLPs) are already buildable, already norm-free, f = 50.000 %, and need
  no patch — rejected on **dynamic range**: an MNIST MLP plateaus near 98 %, so the whole
  error budget is ~1.7 pp against a 0.12 pp seed floor, compressing a ~0.6 pp target effect
  ~5× into the noise, on a task whose pp gaps are not commensurable with the ResNet ones.
  Underpowered, not merely dirty.

**Superseded by `gn1` (§4)**, which is not a consolation prize: it is the tighter test, and
it is the one `PLAN-appendix-paper.md` already registered as required ("ResNet-18 (BN) + a
GroupNorm or ViT variant").

### 5.3 KILLED: Idea C entirely — the count-matched prescription test

*Design:* `nodewise1d` with groups added back by finer chunking of the ndim≥2 tensors to reach
m ≈ 14,420, compared against plain `nodewise`.

**REASON — THE PROPOSED ARM ALREADY EXISTS AND HAS BEEN RUN 18 TIMES UNDER THE NAME
`chunk777`.** Max one-D numel is **512 < 777**, so `⌈numel/777⌉ = 1` for **all 41** one-D
tensors: `chunk777`'s one-D groups are identical to `nodewise1d`'s, **41/41, zero mismatches**.
`chunk777` **is** "nodewise1d with the ndim≥2 tensors re-chunked to restore m to ≈14,420",
group for group. Not similar to it — **it is it**.

So Idea C's contrast is `D`, re-derived within-batch at write time: `mm1` **+0.485** (t 3.01)
/ `pp1` **+0.581** (t 4.11) / `ar1` **+0.697** (t 5.90) / `fa1` **+0.629** (t 5.11) / `cc1`
**+0.727** (t 3.63); **inverse-variance pooled +0.627, se 0.063** across five batches, two
meta-stepsizes, two clip boxes, Δm = 1 group. **The count-matched prescription test has already
returned CONFIRMED, five times.**

**And the count confound it was invented to remove is separately measured and null.** With the
prescription applied on both sides, `chunk777 − nodewise1d` spans exactly the 0.4731 decades of
m at issue (14,421 → 4,851) and reads **−0.109** (`ar1`) / **−0.019** (`fa1`) / **−0.089**
(`cc1`) — about **−0.06 pp**, not the +0.19 pp the uniform-chunk ladder's −0.407 pp/decade
would predict. Consequently the count-**un**matched `H = nodewise1d − nodewise` (+0.427 `bn1`
/ +0.806 `ar1` / +0.649 `fa1` / **+0.816** `cc1`) and the count-**matched** `D` (+0.627) agree
to **0.044 pp — about a third of the 0.12 pp seed-noise median.** The confound is empirically
negligible. (Separately: **+0.816 is the max cell of `H`, not the effect**; the campaign's own
rule forbids quoting it. `H` pooled ≈ +0.67.)

**The "purer" node-aligned fixed-m variant is blocked by a hard identity**, not an engineering
shortfall: nodewise = 4,810 ND + 9,610 one-D groups, so deleting 9,569 tail groups at fixed
m = 14,420 **forces** the ND side to 14,379 groups, ~3× finer. You can never hold both the tail
and the ND partition fixed at fixed m. Nor can it hit 14,420 with any uniform K — the ND
node-slice sizes are {27, 64, 128, 256, 512, 576, 1152, 2304, 4608}, so `⌈gsize/K⌉` jumps
14,195 (K = 922) → 15,731 (K = 768) with nothing between. It would need a new patch and a new
batch to answer a question `G` has already returned null on.

**Cost of the kill: 0 jobs. Saved: ~9–12 jobs on a single-account queue.**

**ONE THING IDEA C DID EXPOSE, AND IT BELONGS IN THE PAPER.** Every chunk arm at K ≥ 512
silently implements the prescription. So the campaign's "uniform beats architecture-aligned"
partition story is, mechanically, "arms that merge the normalisation scalars beat arms that do
not". **The uniform-vs-aligned framing and the tail framing are the same finding read two
ways**, and the paper must say so explicitly rather than presenting `D` and the tail census as
independent evidence.

## 7. WHAT MAY AND MAY NOT BE CLAIMED, TODAY

**MAY**, today, before `gn1` runs — this sentence is **already earned** at fixed count by five
independent batches across two meta-stepsizes and two clip boxes:

> At matched group count (14,421 vs 14,420), merging each BatchNorm scale and shift into a
> single step-size group instead of giving every channel its own is worth **+0.63 pp**
> (IV-pooled +0.627, se 0.063, 18 within-batch seed pairs) of final test accuracy on
> ResNet18/CIFAR-10.

**MAY ALSO**: the census; the structural identity `f = b/(1+b) → 2/3`; the DO-NOT-RUN verdict
on the cross-architecture singleton-fraction test; the observation that every `chunk<K≥512>`
arm silently implements the prescription (§5.3) — which means the campaign's "uniform beats
architecture-aligned" story and its tail story are **the same finding read two ways**, and the
paper must say so rather than presenting them as independent evidence.

**MAY NOT**:

* that the gap **scales with** singleton count, or any dose-response language at all. **This
  is now a permanent prohibition, not a pending one:** `sl1` was the batch that would have
  earned it, and §3 shows the design could not — a fixed-*m* ladder cannot separate singleton
  injection from ndim ≥ 2 coarsening at any n, and its slope scored CONFIRMED for a pure step
  and a non-monotone zigzag. No dose-response claim may be made until the §3.4 successor is
  registered, built and run.
* that the prescription generalises to **normalisation layers** rather than to **BatchNorm** —
  that is exactly what `gn1` is for, and **a null from `gn1` alone does not license the
  converse narrowing either** (§4.7, three readings).
* `gap(f)` in any functional form, any predicted rung value, or any extrapolation.
* **+0.816** as "the effect" of the prescription (it is the max cell of `H`; pooled ≈ +0.67,
  and the count-matched form is +0.627).
* `fa1`'s +0.629 without §2.3's ceiling caveat attached.
* any `D` sentence that does not carry its box, and no pooling of `gn1`'s numbers with
  `mm1`/`pp1`/`cc1`/`ar1`/`fa1`.

**AND THE STANDING GENERALISATION DEBT, WHICH THIS CYCLE MADE MORE URGENT, NOT LESS:** the
tail is still **one architecture and one dataset** — 48 runs, all ResNet18/CIFAR-10, and zero
elsewhere. `gn1` discharges the *normalisation* axis only. The three built generalisation
batches outrank it (§6.0) and now have a new number to carry on every branch: the count
confound at this *m* range is measured at **≈ −0.06 pp** (`chunk777 − nodewise1d` = −0.109 /
−0.019 / −0.089), not the +0.19 pp the uniform ladder extrapolation predicts.
